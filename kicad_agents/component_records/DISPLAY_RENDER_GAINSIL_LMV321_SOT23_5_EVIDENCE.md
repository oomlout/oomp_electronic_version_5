# Gainsil LMV321-TR SOT-23-5 display evidence

The Gainsil-authored [datasheet mirrored by LCSC](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/lcsc/2001131822_Gainsil-LMV321-TR_C362273.pdf), ordering table, identifies LMV321-TR as SOT23-5 and lists `321` as its marking. Typeface, orientation, placement and additional production-dependent marks are illustrative. Catalogue dimensions list 2.92 × 2.8 mm and the SOT-23-5 footprint but omit height. The generic KiCad model is vertically scaled from 1.55 to 1.45 mm based on an existing display pilot; that height is not verified for this part and the model is not a verified mechanical or PCB model.
