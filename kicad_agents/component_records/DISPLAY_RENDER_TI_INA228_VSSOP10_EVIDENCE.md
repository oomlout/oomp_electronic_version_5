# TI INA228 VSSOP-10 family display research

The catalogue ID implies Texas Instruments INA228, but its manufacturer and exact orderable fields are missing and no footprint is selected. Its dimensions are 4.9 × 3.0 × 1.1 mm. The [TI INA228 data sheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ina228.pdf) specifies DGS0010A: 2.9–3.1 mm square body, 4.75–5.05 mm overall lead span, 0.5 mm pitch and 1.1 mm maximum height. It lists the VSSOP-10 orderable INA228AIDGSR with fixed marking `228I`; this is a representative family marking, not evidence that the generic catalogue entry names that exact orderable. Logo, font, placement and variable production characters are illustrative.

The already-inspected KiCad `Package_SO.3dshapes/MSOP-10_3x3mm_P0.5mm.step` has SHA-256 `b8f88eac3ce221c2ccb0b5048f9ad49c2ef4af148f8ae50c4e232e357683f547` and 4.9 × 3.0 × 1.1 mm overall bounds, compatible with the TI package drawing for display. The catalogue's separate package drawing instead treats 4.9 mm as body length, has a 6.2 mm overall span and spaces its pins 0.6 mm apart; these differ from the TI package drawing and must be disclosed. This is not a verified footprint or mechanical model.
