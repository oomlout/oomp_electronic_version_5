# Honor RVT 10 × 10.2 mm display candidates

The Honor RVT series specification viewed online
(https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf,
pages 4–6) calls for capacitance, voltage and negative-polarity marking,
and lists the 10 × 10.2 mm family. Individual supplier pages confirm:

- RVT1H221M1010 / C3358: 220 µF 50 V, https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/HonorElec-RVT1H221M1010/C3358
- RVT1E471M1010 / C3351: 470 µF 25 V, https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/HonorElec-RVT1E471M1010/C3351
- RVT1V471M1010 / C3350: 470 µF 35 V, https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/HonorElec-RVT1V471M1010/C3350

The KiCad polarized model is scaled to 10.2 mm overall; its dark sector is
on negative pad 2. Numeric text, metal finish, stripe shape and proportions
are illustrative. Exact ink, maker logo and date are unverified. No PDF was
downloaded for this batch.
