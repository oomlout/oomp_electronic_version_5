# Broadcom HCPL-0600-500E SO-8 display evidence

The Broadcom-authored [datasheet](https://docs.rs-online.com/2e1c/0900766b8163d613.pdf) includes HCPL-0600, SO-8 dimensions of 5.080 × 3.937 mm body, 3.175 mm height, 5.994 mm lead span and 1.27 mm pitch. [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/broadcom-limited/HCPL-0600-500E/669919) identifies the exact orderable. Older package drawings identify the type with the last three digits; newer drawings indicate a four-character device number. The render uses 0600 as an illustrative device identifier; manufacturer marks, date and lot codes are omitted, and typography and layout vary.

The catalogue specifies 4.9 × 3.9 mm and omits height. The procedural display model follows nominal body and lead dimensions from the manufacturer. The individual lead profiles, chamfers and pin-one dot are approximations; this is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
