# JSCJ D882 SOT-89 display research

The catalogue identifies JSCJ D882, gain range 160–320, SOT-89-3L. The locally stored PDF (SHA-256 `52bef9995ece4200a861c2b940a93862e72f076631151d985d45c7d19addd995`) is a Jiangsu Changjiang Electronics Technology (JCST) document, not a JSCJ document, and cannot prove the JSCJ package marking.

An independently viewed [JSCJ D882 SOT-89-3L revision 2.1 datasheet](https://pdf.voron.ua/files/pdf/CJ/Transistors/D882_SOT-89-3L.pdf) identifies the device and Y gain class 160–320, but does not specify the physical top marking. The related [JSCJ D882 TO-126 datasheet](https://pdf.voron.ua/files/pdf/CJ/Transistors/D882_TO_126_V2.pdf) shows D882 as a device code in a different package. Therefore the SOT-89 render's D882 text is an illustrative identifier, not a verified marking; typeface, placement and any production or grade markings are unverified. The online files were read without saving replacement PDFs.

The KiCad SOT-89 display model's 4.5 × 2.5 mm body, 4.0 mm overall width and 1.52 mm height fit the maker's SOT-89 outline. The uncoloured STEP is assigned body and metal roles by the checked geometry adapter. Its material finish and other details are approximate, and it is not verified manufacturing CAD.
