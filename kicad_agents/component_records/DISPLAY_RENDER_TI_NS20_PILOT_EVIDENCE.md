# TI SN74HC374NSR NS-20 display pilot evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [TI's exact orderable page](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74HC374/part-details/SN74HC374NSR) identifies the SOP NS, 20-pin package. Its [datasheet ordering table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc374.pdf) lists package mark `HC374`. The [TI NS drawing linked from that exact product page](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/MSOP002A), visually reviewed in Chrome, specifies a 20-pin body length of 12.30–12.90 mm, width 5.00–5.60 mm, lead span 7.40–8.20 mm, 1.27 mm pitch and 2.00 mm maximum seated height. The product datasheet body-size summary lists 15 x 5.3 mm, inconsistent with this drawing; see DISPLAY_RENDER_TI_NS20_RESEARCH.md. Local record's 7.2 x 5.3 x 1.98 mm and 0.65 mm pitch SSOP footprint do not match the manufacturer's linked NS drawing. No source data is corrected here.

The KiCad SOIC-20W 7.5 x 12.8 mm, 1.27 mm pitch model is a display approximation scaled across width to 75% and vertically from 2.75 to 2.00 mm. Its resulting overall span is 7.725 mm; approximate scaled body width is 5.625 mm, 0.025 mm above the NS drawing's maximum. Body length remains 12.8 mm. This is not a verified mechanical model or footprint. Typeface, orientation, placement, pin-one indicator and variable manufacturer codes are illustrative. Inspect the preview and full image before publication.
