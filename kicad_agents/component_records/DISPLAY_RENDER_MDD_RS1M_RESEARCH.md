# MDD RS1M SMA display research

The local MDD family datasheet SHA-256 `afe6d0c403d11d40302b82decde3f845fa1750cd495875017f4635cc5ae6c30f` page 1 was rendered
and visually reviewed. It identifies RS1M as DO-214AC/SMA and confirms a
cathode band. The table does not establish the actual package text marking;
RS1M on the render is an illustrative identifier only.

KiCad `D_SMA.step`, SHA-256
`1cfddd4e3b1bf22b8fc6ee3258c2aade882ed7b85b528e23b9db42437775a1db`,
has 5.0 × 2.7 × 2.22 mm overall dimensions; its body exceeds the drawing's
4.5 mm maximum by approximately 0.06 mm. The source record's 10 × 5 mm
dimensions and missing footprint conflict with the drawing. The display is
an approximation, not manufacturer CAD or a PCB footprint. No PDF was downloaded.

The catalogue manufacturer field says `RS1M (SMTonly)`; the datasheet
uses `RS1M`. The parenthetical is retained in the source as a descriptor.
