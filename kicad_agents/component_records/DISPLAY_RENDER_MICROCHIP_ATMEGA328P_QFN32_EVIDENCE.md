# Microchip ATmega328P-MU QFN-32 display research

Microchip's [ATmega328/P data sheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/cn/DeviceDoc/cn604759.pdf) maps `ATmega328P-MU` to package 32M1-A, a 5 × 5 × 1.0 mm 32-pad QFN with 0.5 mm pitch. A [manufacturer package drawing](https://www.microchip.com/content/dam/mchp/documents/OTH/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/1062S.pdf) for 32M1-A gives a 3.10 mm nominal exposed pad, range 2.95–3.25 mm. The catalogue's 5 × 5 × 1.0 mm dimensions agree, but its manufacturer, exact orderable and footprint fields are absent; Microchip and the orderable are inferred from the ID.

The closest installed KiCad candidate is `QFN-32-1EP_5x5mm_P0.5mm_EP3.3x3.3mm.step`, SHA-256 `5fa713ea46b36980895812ed853577889b7cb06d275041a39b99bc0be305dc79`. Inspected bounds are 5 × 5 × 0.875 mm, with coating, termination and pin-indicator colours. Its nominal exposed pad is 3.3 mm, 0.05 mm larger than the manufacturer's stated maximum, so it is an approximate display model. The source's separate package drawing gives 6.2 mm overall width and pin positions at 0.625 mm intervals, inconsistent with the manufacturer drawing's 5 mm body and 0.5 mm pitch. `MEGA328P` is an illustrative family identifier; exact top marking, logo, date, lot, typeface and placement are unverified. This display is not a verified mechanical model or footprint.
