# Fenghua RC-02 5% 0402 display evidence

Fenghua's general chip resistor approval sheet R-6.1 was reviewed online:
https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/8d58778925076bf80f4c3e6ed51a56f5.pdf?productCode=C115306

The sheet lists RC-02 and specifies that 0402 glass sides have no marking.
Its ordering system identifies J as 5% tolerance and the three-digit value
code. Its dimensions give a nominal 1.00 × 0.50 × 0.30 mm body; the KiCad
model's nominal height of 0.35 mm is within the specified tolerance.
The image uses generic geometry and illustrative materials, and represents
the selected supplier, not every possible equivalent. This review does not
establish the marking for RS-02, which the sheet does not list.

The manufacturer sheet was inspected online; no local PDF is claimed.
