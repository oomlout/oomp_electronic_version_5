# Microchip ATmega328P-AU TQFP-32 display evidence

[Microchip's product page](https://www.microchip.com/en-us/product/ATmega328p) lists ATMEGA328P-AU in the 32-lead TQFP package. The [ATmega328P family datasheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/ATmega48A-PA-88A-PA-168A-PA-328-P-DS-DS40002061A.pdf) identifies the 32A package as 7 × 7 mm body, 1.0 mm body thickness and 0.8 mm pitch. The related [ATmega328P automotive package drawing](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/Atmel-7810-Automotive-Microcontrollers-ATmega328P_Datasheet.pdf) shows 9.0 mm nominal lead span and 1.20 mm maximum overall height. The catalogue's dimensions are 9 × 9 × 1.2 mm and its footprint agrees with this family.

The KiCad TQFP-32_7x7mm_P0.8mm STEP model has 9 × 9 × 1.1 mm bounds, including leads, and the expected body, lead and pin indicator materials. `MEGA328P` is an illustrative identifier: Microchip shows that identifier for the related automotive package in its [errata](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MCU08/ProductDocuments/Errata/ATmega328P-Auto-SilConErrataClarif-DS80001060.pdf), but the exact marking of this non-automotive orderable, placement, typeface, logo, date and lot are not verified. The display is not a verified mechanical model or footprint.
