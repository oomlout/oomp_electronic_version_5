# Honor RVT 6.3 × 5.4 mm 220 µF 10 V display pilot

Reviewed 2026-11-18. [LCSC C3345](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3345.html)
identifies Honor RVT1A221M0605 as polarized 220 µF, 10 V, 6.3 mm diameter
and 5.4 mm seated height. The [Honor RVT specification](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf)
pages 4–6 calls for value, voltage and negative polarity and lists 6.3 ×
5.4 mm geometry. `220` / `10V` text is illustrative; exact ink layout,
maker logo and production code are not verified.

KiCad CP_Elec_6.3x5.4 has a dark top sector on positive X, aligned to
negative pad 2 of the corresponding footprint and polarized symbol. Metal
finish, sector and proportions are illustrative. No PDF was downloaded.
