All five pages describe TO-220AB and show an unrelated IRF1010 marking
example; the device identifiers in the display are therefore illustrative,
with exact top marking, logo, typeface and variable lot/date codes unverified.
The KiCad TO-220-3 display model is 10.25 x 4.455 x 28.525 mm overall and
its body-to-tip lead projection is approximately 13.435 mm. IRF3415 and
IRFB260N specify 13.47–14.09 mm, so the model is approximately 0.035 mm
shorter than their drawing minimum. Source dimensions are generic; shape and
finish are approximate and the display is not verified manufacturing CAD.
