# Chengx GR 1000 µF / 10 V, 8 × 12 mm display pilot

Reviewed 2026-11-19. The [manufacturer's exact GR108M010F12RR0VL4FP0
specification](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/chengxing_1000uf10v_Lcs01_link.pdf)
identifies a 1000 µF / 10 V, 8 × 12 mm part with **green body and gold
lettering** (`绿体金字`). [LCSC C3299](https://item.szlcsc.com/3690.html)
identifies 3.5 mm lead spacing and the same dimensions and ratings.

Use green sleeve and gold-coloured print for this part. The precise shade,
metallic appearance, placement, negative stripe, letter shapes and text
`1000uF / 10V / GR 105C` are illustrative; the manufacturer wording does
not establish their exact design. The KiCad 8 mm P3.5 model has a straight
sleeve extended to 12 mm, as in the already reviewed KM example, and the
negative stripe must align with footprint pad 2. Inspect materials, stripe,
geometry and each preview before publication. No PDF was downloaded.
