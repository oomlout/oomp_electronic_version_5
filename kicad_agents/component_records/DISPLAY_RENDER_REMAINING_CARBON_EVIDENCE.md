# Remaining carbon-film resistor displays

Reviewed 2026-11-18. The existing catalogue source records preserve supplier
listing descriptions for LCSC C5200377 (39 Ω, 250 mW, ±5%, 6.5 × 2.4 mm)
and C337041 (39 kΩ, 125 mW, ±5%, 3.7 × 2.3 mm). Their saved manufacturer
numbers are truncated (`T/B` and `STB5`); those are not full part identities.
The exact supplier pages were unavailable during this review. A TyoHM
[RD 1/4W 39R J T/B listing](https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/9821465.html) is a
plausible similar part but its identity as C5200377 was not established.
Do not silently replace either supplier selection or claim an exact MPN.

Standard four-band value representations are orange/white/black/gold for
39 Ω and orange/white/orange/gold for 39 kΩ. Exact manufacturer's band count,
placement and body colour are unverified. Blue is an illustrative display
finish. KiCad DIN0207 6.3 × 2.5 mm approximates the 39 Ω listing; DIN0204
3.6 × 1.6 mm approximates the 39 kΩ body length but is substantially thinner.
The 39 kΩ selected rating and body disagree with the existing quarter-watt ID
and larger footprint; those records need review. Formed model pitch is 7.62 mm.
