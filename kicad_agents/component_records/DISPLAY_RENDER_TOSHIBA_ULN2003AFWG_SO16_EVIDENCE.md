# Toshiba ULN2003AFWG SOL16 display evidence

Toshiba's [product table](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/tw/semiconductor/product/linear-ics/transistor-arrays.html) and [manufacturer-authored datasheet mirrored by DigiKey](https://media.digikey.com/pdf/Data%20Sheets/Toshiba%20PDFs/ULN2003APG%2C%20AFWG%20and%20ULN2004APG%2C%20AFWG.pdf) identify ULN2003AFWG in SOL16-P-150-1.27A. The datasheet drawing gives 9.91 +0.07/-0.11 mm length, 3.94 +0.15/-0.13 mm body width, 5.99 ±0.15 mm lead span and 1.73 mm maximum height. Exact top marking was not established; `ULN2003A` is an illustrative family identifier. Typeface, orientation and placement are illustrative; logos and variable date/lot markings are omitted.

The catalogue lists 9.9 × 3.9 mm without height or footprint. The generic KiCad SOIC-16 model is vertically scaled from 1.75 to 1.70 mm to approximate the package height; it is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
