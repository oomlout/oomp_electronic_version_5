# Huaxing MF quarter-watt display evidence

Reviewed online on 2026-11-17: the manufacturer-authored [MF/RJ metal film
specification](https://xonstorage.blob.core.windows.net/pdf/huaxing_mf14w1k1t52_apr22_xonlink.pdf),
section 4, gives five bands for 1%: three significant digits, multiplier,
and brown tolerance band. Section 5 gives normal MF1/4W dimensions 6.5 ± 1 mm
long and 2.3 ± 0.5 mm diameter. The selected KiCad model's nominal 6.3 by
2.5 mm body falls within these tolerances; its leads are bent for the chosen
7.62 mm footprint.

The specification states a blue body. The user requested traditional beige,
so this render deliberately uses an illustrative beige body; the particular
finish, stripe spacing and shades are not a production photograph. Manufacturer
PDF reviewed online only; no local copy is claimed. Verify each exact MPN,
value, tolerance and supplier record before rendering.
