# Honor RVT 8 × 10.5 mm 220 µF 25 V display pilot

Reviewed 2026-11-18. [LCSC C3353](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3353.html)
identifies Honor RVT1E221M0810 as polarized 220 µF 25 V, with a package
designation 8 × 10.5 mm, but its seated-height field says 10.2 mm. A
[manufacturer datasheet mirror](https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/6931683.html)
lists the exact RVT1E221M0810 as 8 × 10.5 mm; the separate
[series drawing](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf)
gives 8 × 10.2 mm ±0.5 mm. The relationship between these specifications is
not established. This illustration uses the source record and manufacturer
part table's 10.5 mm geometry and explicitly discloses the conflicting
supplier height. It is not dimensional purchasing or fit guidance.

The RVT series calls for value, voltage and negative polarity marking.
`220` / `25V` is an illustrative layout; exact ink, logo and date are
unverified. KiCad CP_Elec_8x10.5 has a dark sector on positive X, aligned
with negative pad 2. Metal finish, sector and proportions are illustrative.
