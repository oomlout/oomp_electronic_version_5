# TyoHM 5% quarter-watt axial displays

Reviewed online on 2026-11-18. The existing supplier choice is retained.

* LCSC [C433702](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C433702.html) identifies
  RD 1/4W 5M6 J T/B A1, 5.6 MΩ, ±5%, 250 mW and 6.5 × 2.4 mm.
* LCSC [C5200510](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C5200510.html) identifies
  RT 1/4W 2M2 J T/B, 2.2 MΩ, ±5%, 250 mW and 6.4 × 2.4 mm.
  The [TyoHM RT series specification](https://xonstorage.blob.core.windows.net/pdf/tyohm_rt14w1mftb_apr22_xonlink.pdf)
  specifies J as ±5%, colour coding and light green coating on quarter-watt parts.

The standard 5% four-band value codes are green/blue/green/gold and
red/red/green/gold respectively. Exact manufacturer band count, spacing and
colour shades were not confirmed for each part. The user's requested beige body
is illustrative, including for the light-green RT part. The KiCad 6.3 × 2.5 mm
model approximates both supplier dimensions; its bent leads are 7.62 mm apart.
No manufacturer PDF has been saved locally as part of this review.
