# Infineon IR2110STRPBF wide SOIC-16 display evidence

Infineon [product information](https://www.infineon.com/part/IR2110S) identifies this exact orderable as SOIC 16W. Its [IR2110/IR2113 datasheet](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/49/infineon-ir2110-datasheet-en.pdf), case outline page 16, gives body 10.10–10.50 × 7.40–7.60 mm, lead span 10.00–10.65 mm, pitch 1.27 mm and height 2.35–2.65 mm. Catalogue 9.9 × 3.9 mm is inconsistent with that wide body; height and footprint are unspecified. The generic KiCad SOIC-16W model has 10.3 × 10.3 × 2.75 mm overall bounds, and is scaled vertically to 2.50 mm for a display approximation, not a verified footprint.

`IR2110` is an illustrative partial identifier; the exact physical top marking is not established, and suffix, logo and variable date/lot codes are omitted. Typography, placement and orientation are illustrative.
