# JSCJ BAT54C SOT-23 display evidence

The local PDF at
`parts_source/electronic_diode_schottky_dual_common_cathode_sot_23_jiangsu_changjing_electronics_technology_co_ltd_bat54c/datasheet.pdf`,
SHA-256 `4cc9a95188fa1f0bdeb4d3242fb1ec60d82cc4cfa9b27c90dcc819e8eaf97de4`,
was reviewed 2026-11-21. Its first page shows BAT54C in SOT-23 and `KL3`, but
it is branded JCST / Jiangsu Changjiang, whereas the catalogue manufacturer is
JSCJ / Jiangsu Changjing. Consequently the local PDF does **not** verify the
exact JSCJ marking. `KL3` is an illustrative family example from the different
manufacturer; exact JSCJ code, typeface and production details are unverified.

The online [JSCJ BAT54 family sheet](https://www.jscj-elec.com/gallery/file/BAT54%20A%20C%20S%20SOT-23%20V2.0.pdf)
describes SOT-23 and its page 3 gives 2.80–3.00 mm length, 2.25–2.55 mm
overall lead span and 0.90–1.15 mm height. The separately reviewed local JSCJ
BAW56 family sheet has the same SOT-23 outline. The KiCad SOT-23 model is
2.5 x 3.0 x 1.2 mm overall and scaled to 1.1 mm height for display. This is
an illustrative display model, not a manufacturing model or PCB footprint.
