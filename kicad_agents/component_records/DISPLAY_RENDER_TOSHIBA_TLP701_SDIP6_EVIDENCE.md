# Toshiba TLP701(TP,F) surface-mount SDIP-6 display evidence

[Toshiba's TLP701 datasheet](https://toshiba-semicon-storage.com/info/TLP701_datasheet_en_20190925.pdf?did=1642&prodName=TLP701) specifies SDIP6 package 11-5J1S with 6.8 × 4.58 mm nominal body, 4.0 mm nominal height, 1.27 mm lead pitch and 9.7 mm nominal overall span. [A distributor listing](https://www.tme.eu/en/details/tlp701-tp.f/optocouplers-digital-output/toshiba/tlp701-tp-f/) identifies exact TLP701(TP,F) as surface-mount SDIP6. The render uses TLP701 as an illustrative family identifier; exact marking, typography, logo and variable codes are not verified.

The catalogue source gives 10 × 5 mm with no dimension reference, height or footprint. The procedural display model uses nominal body and span; its formed-lead geometry, chamfers and pin-one indicator are approximations, and the exact lead variant is not confirmed by the manufacturer drawing. It is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
