The N (R-PDIP) mechanical data specify a 7.62 mm nominal row pitch; the catalogue specifies 19.2 × 6.35 × 3.3 mm and a DIP-14 W7.62 mm footprint. The full model bounds include leads and are not directly comparable to catalogue body height. It uses a moulded notch for pin-one orientation, not a separate colored dot.
