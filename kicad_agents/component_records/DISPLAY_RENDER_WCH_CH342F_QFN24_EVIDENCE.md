# WCH CH342F QFN24 display evidence

[WCH's CH342 datasheet](https://docs.sparkfun.com/SparkFun_GNSS_LG580P/assets/component_documentation/CH342%20Datasheet.pdf) identifies CH342F as QFN24 with 4 × 4 mm body, 0.5 mm pitch and an exposed ground pad. The source record names WCH CH342F in the catalogue ID but has no manufacturer or part number fields; identity is inferred. Its source note claims no exposed centre pad, contradicting the manufacturer and its own 1EP footprint.

KiCad's generic QFN24 model has 4 × 4 × 0.77 mm bounds and 2.7 × 2.7 mm exposed pad. The source lists 0.8 mm height but the manufacturer pad dimensions and height are not independently confirmed. CH342F is an illustrative identifier, not a verified top marking; logo, date and lot codes are omitted. This is a display approximation, not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
