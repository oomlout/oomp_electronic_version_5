# Infineon P-channel DPAK display research

The following distinct catalogue records have local International Rectifier
datasheets. Each page 1 identifies its IRFR surface-mount device, and each
page 8 TO-252AA drawing was visually reviewed.

| IRFR9120NTRPBF | `1d50f1df07903a07cbbb9e06b0d80ba1129366c1aac17621473bd69ff21ba540` |

Each drawing shows two full outer leads, a short centre lead and a metal tab.
The KiCad TO-252-2 model is a closer display approximation than the
three-full-lead model associated with the unchanged TO-252-3_TabPin2 PCB
footprint. Its 9.9 x 6.5 x 2.32 mm overall bounds and centre stub are
approximate; it is not a verified mechanical model or footprint.

All three marking illustrations use an unrelated IRFR120 example. `IRFR5305`,
`IRFR9024N` and `IRFR9120N` are illustrative partial identifiers only; exact
top markings, logos, typeface, placement and production codes are unverified.
