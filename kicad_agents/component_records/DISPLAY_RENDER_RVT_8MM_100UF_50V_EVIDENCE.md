# Honor RVT 8 mm 100 µF 50 V display pilot

Reviewed 2026-11-18. [LCSC C3352](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3352.html)
identifies Honor RVT1H101M0810 as polarized 100 µF, 50 V, 8 mm diameter,
10.2 mm seated height. Its source record links a local PDF path which is
currently absent; no PDF was downloaded.

The [manufacturer RVT series specification for another value](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf)
pages 4–6 describes capacitance, voltage and negative polarity marking and the
8 × 10.2 mm case family. Existing local 10 mm RVT series documentation has an
illustrative photograph of metal cans with a dark top sector. These sources do
not establish the exact appearance of this individual unit. Thus `100` and
`50V` as two lines are illustrative, with no invented maker logo or date code.

The KiCad polarized 8 × 10 model has a dark top sector on its pad-2 side; the
corresponding KiCad symbol identifies pin 1 as positive, so the sector is on
the negative side. The model's 10.0 mm height will be scaled to the cited
10.2 mm seated height for display only, including its base. Finish, exact ink
layout, model proportions and polarity-sector shape may vary in production.
No exact product photo or manufacturer marking layout was verified.
