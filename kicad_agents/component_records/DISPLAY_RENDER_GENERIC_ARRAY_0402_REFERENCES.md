# UNI-ROYAL visual references for unsourced 4×0402 resistor arrays

The generic entries specify four independent 0402-size resistors and eight
pins, with a nominal resistance but no selected supplier or tolerance. Images
illustrate the named 5% UNI-ROYAL examples without assigning their supplier or
tolerance to the entries. KiCad geometry, materials, typeface and ink placement
are illustrative.

three-digit 5% markings:
