# Yageo RT1206DRE07133KL display evidence

[JLCPCB C706443](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/YAGEO-RT1206DRE07133KL/C706443)
identifies the selected Yageo RT1206DRE07133KL as 133 kΩ, 0.5%, 1206,
0.25 W thin film. The [Yageo RT series specification](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/46d7760c582640714d605308a25a220a.pdf?productCode=C1702657)
shows RT1206 E24/E96 values with four digits: three significant figures
and a decimal exponent. The 133 kΩ marking is therefore `1333`.

The KiCad display geometry, colour, materials, typeface and positioning are
illustrative and do not assert a production-lot appearance. The specification
was reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed.
