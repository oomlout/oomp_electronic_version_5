# YAGEO RC_L chip resistor display evidence

Reviewed online: YAGEO RC_L series product specification, version 12,
2 August 2022, hosted as a manufacturer-authored PDF by a distributor:
https://robu-prod-media.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2025/08/C137992.pdf

- Page 2 defines the RC part-number fields, tolerances, reel codes and
  resistance value notation, including R, K and M as decimal markers.
- Page 3 identifies RC0201 and RC0402 as unmarked.
- Page 4 gives nominal RC0201 dimensions 0.60 × 0.30 × 0.23 mm and RC0402
  1.00 × 0.50 × 0.35 mm; the KiCad models use those nominal dimensions.
- Page 5 specifies resistance limits by size and tolerance. The adapter must
  check the exact primary part number, value, footprint and supplier entry.

The evidence was reviewed online; a local manufacturer PDF has not been
captured. The illustration uses generic KiCad geometry and materials and is
not a photograph or a guarantee of a particular production lot's appearance.
Other series, including AF and AC, require their own evidence.
