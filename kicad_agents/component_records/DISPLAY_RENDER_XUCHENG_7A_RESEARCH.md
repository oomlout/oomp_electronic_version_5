# Xucheng descriptive 7 A glass fuse display research

The source's manufacturer part number `7A 250VACGlass Tube Fuse` is a
descriptive string. [LCSC C3121](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3121.html)
lists a Xucheng 7 A, 250 V, nominal 5.2 × 20 mm glass tube. This part has no
local PDF and its exact fuse series, time characteristic and production cap
marking are not established by that listing. Do not infer a fast or slow code.

The illustration may use the existing 5.2 × 20 mm hollow glass model and an
illustrative `7A 250V` cap label based on the distributor rating. The label,
glass thickness, cap size, wire and finish are approximate. The source's
6.5 mm height conflicts with the nominal cylindrical diameter. The image is
not manufacturer CAD or a PCB footprint. No PDF was downloaded.
