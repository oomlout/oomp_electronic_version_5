# Toshiba TC7S SOT-25 display evidence

Toshiba identifies [TC7SU04F](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/eu/semiconductor/product/general-purpose-logic-ics/detail.TC7SU04F.html) and [TC7S32F](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/us/semiconductor/product/general-purpose-logic-ics/detail.TC7S32F.html) as SOT-25 (SMV); the second product page lists 2.9 × 2.8 × 1.1 mm overall package dimensions. Source records list 2.9 × 1.6 mm and SOT-23-5 footprint, with no height. Generic KiCad model is scaled vertically from 1.55 to 1.1 mm as a display approximation, not a verified footprint.

The [TC7SU04F datasheet](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/info/TC7SU04F_datasheet_en_20170802.pdf?did=20190&prodName=TC7SU04F) depicts E6; the [TC7S32F datasheet](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/info/TC7S32F_datasheet_en_20140301.pdf?did=20184&prodName=TC7S32F) depicts E4 and identifies 4 as the product name. These are examples of depicted markings; production-dependent characters, exact typography, orientation and placement are not verified.
