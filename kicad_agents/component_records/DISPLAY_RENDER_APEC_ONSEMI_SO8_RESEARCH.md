The KiCad SOIC-8 display model has 6.0 x 4.9 x 1.75 mm overall bounds and
four gullwing leads on each side. The APEC outline gives 4.8–5.0 mm body
length, 5.8–6.5 mm lead span and up to 1.75 mm height. The fixed device codes
are documented; typeface, logo, placement and variable production details
are illustrative or omitted. Display only, not a verified manufacturing
model or PCB footprint.
