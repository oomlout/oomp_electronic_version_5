# VO MF quarter-watt display evidence

The manufacturer-authored [MF metal film approval sheet](https://www.unikeyic.com/media/datasheet/03/d5/0807/03/a28bc90a817595158c1877f2e10ceabc.pdf),
reviewed online on 2026-11-17, describes the MF series as using MIL/EIA colour
codes and states that normal-size bodies are khaki. It gives the 1/4 W body as
6.2 ± 1 mm long and 2.7 ± 1 mm diameter; the selected 6.3 by 2.5 mm KiCad
body is within that range. The model depicts leads bent to the chosen 7.62 mm
footprint.

The manufacturer sheet does not specify the number of bands for each MPN.
We use a five-band representation inferred from the standard 1% code (three
significant digits, multiplier, brown tolerance), also illustrated by the
[Huaxing MF marking table](https://xonstorage.blob.core.windows.net/pdf/huaxing_mf14w1k1t52_apr22_xonlink.pdf).
The exact band count and layout of a VO production lot are not verified; shade,
spacing and the traditional beige finish are illustrative. No local PDF is
claimed. Check exact MPN, resistance, tolerance, footprint and supplier data.
