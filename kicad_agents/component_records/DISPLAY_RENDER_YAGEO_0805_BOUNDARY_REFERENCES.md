# 0805 Yageo reference parts for 110 Ω and 10 MΩ

The selected suppliers are Fenghua RS-05K111JT and UNI-ROYAL 0805W8F1005T5E.
The latter lies at the boundary of the previously reviewed UNI-ROYAL evidence;
selected-part markings have not been independently verified for these entries.
Images illustrate the named Yageo references, with KiCad geometry, materials,
ink and placement treated as illustrative. Matching nominal resistance,
tolerance and package does not establish complete interchangeability.

The selected 110 Ω part is 5%, according to [JLCPCB C1335](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/1687-RS05K111JT/C1335)
and [LCSC C1335](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1335.html); the source
record's earlier 1% field was corrected. The Yageo 110 Ω, 5% reference
[RC0805JR-07110RL](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C246036.html) is
marked `111` under the Yageo series marking rule.

The selected 10 MΩ 1% part is [0805W8F1005T5E](https://www.es.co.th/detail.asp?Prod=015019922).
The Yageo 10 MΩ 1% reference [RC0805FR-0710ML](https://yageogroup.com/component-documentation/download/specsheet/RC0805FR-0710ML)
is marked `1005` under the series rule. [Yageo RC_L specification](https://yageogroup.com/content/datasheet/asset/file/PYU-RC_GROUP_51_ROHS_L)
page 3 documents 0805 5% three-digit and 1% four-digit codes. Online sources
were read, not saved as local PDFs. The local Fenghua family PDF is separate.
