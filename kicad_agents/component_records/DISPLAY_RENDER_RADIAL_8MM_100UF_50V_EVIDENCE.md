# Radial 8 × 12 mm 100 µF 50 V display pilot

Reviewed 2026-11-18. [LCSC C2749](https://item.szlcsc.com/3130.html)
identifies CX Dongguan Chengxing KM107M050F12RR0VH2FP0 as polarized
100 µF 50 V, 8 mm diameter, 12 mm body height and 3.5 mm lead spacing.
The [manufacturer KM series document for a different value](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/lcsc/1810271410_CX-Dongguan-Chengxing-Elec-KM226M050D11RR0VH2FP0_C43346.pdf)
describes repeated sleeve capacitance, voltage, negative stripe and series
marking. `100uF / 50V / KM 105C` is an illustrative layout; exact font,
logo, date and production artwork are not established. Black sleeve and white
ink are illustrative until the exact product's colour coding is independently
verified. The negative stripe is on pad 2 of the KiCad polarized footprint.

KiCad `CP_Radial_D8.0mm_P3.50mm.step` has an 8 mm body; the display adapter
lengthens only its straight sleeve to 12 mm and leaves the leads/end details
unscaled. Actual crimp, proportions, sleeve stripe, leads and finish may
differ. It is display-only, not dimensional or purchasing guidance. No PDF
was downloaded.
