# CCO MOF3WS large-body display evidence

The selected [47 Ω / C121800](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C121800.html) and [150 kΩ / C121884](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C121884.html) are 3 W, 5% metal-oxide parts with nominal 15.5 × 5 mm bodies. The full manufacturer numbers were restored from those supplier listings; the previous source contained only `T`.

The [CCO MOF specification](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/50533f6f0b2d5d358c34d4d3c0ba8e4c.pdf?productCode=C122053) documents the body dimensions and four-band 5% code: yellow/violet/black/gold for 47 Ω; brown/green/yellow/gold for 150 kΩ. Beige coating and stripe spacing are illustrative; the manufacturer specifies gray or pink for MOF3WS.

The generic KiCad DIN0516 model matches the nominal body with leads formed at 20.32 mm. Existing quarter-watt IDs and smaller 7.62 mm footprints and dimensions require separate catalogue review. The specification was read online; no local PDF is claimed.
