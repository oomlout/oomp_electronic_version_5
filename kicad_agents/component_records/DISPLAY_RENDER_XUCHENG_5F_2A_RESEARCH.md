# Xucheng 5F.2000210000R1 glass fuse display research

The existing local manufacturer PDF (SHA-256
`eed9cc6991947ce18ab00afc8f4fb6e2f727c69e5cc3a9125ea6945fc8681681`)
was visually reviewed. Page 1 depicts a 20 ± 0.5 mm long, 5.2 ± 0.2 mm
diameter cylindrical glass fuse, with metal caps and a visible fuse element.
Page 2 identifies transparent glass, nickel-plated caps and an alloy element,
and specifies the cap marking `F2L250V`. Page 9 associates `2000` with 2 A.
The source record's 6.5 mm height is inconsistent with a 5.2 ± 0.2 mm
cylindrical diameter; preserve the source and disclose the conflict.

To render this well, model the tube as a real transparent glass shell with a
visible wire inside and separate metal caps; a solid opaque cylinder is not an
acceptable representation. Cap marking is on the end caps and needs an
appropriate end-face marking projection. The drawing does not establish exact
typeface and positioning, cap length, glass wall
thickness, internal wire diameter or finish; disclose these as approximations.
This is display geometry, not manufacturing CAD or a PCB footprint.
