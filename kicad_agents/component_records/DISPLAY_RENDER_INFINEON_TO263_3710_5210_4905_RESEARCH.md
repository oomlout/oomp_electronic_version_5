Each drawing shows two full leads, a clipped centre connection and a tab. The KiCad model is adapted for display by clipping the centre lead to an approximate 0.5 mm external stub; its 15 × 10 × 4.42 mm overall bounds and approximately 9.25 × 10 × 4.4 mm body fit the major drawing ranges. Lead-tip details, stub length, material and finish remain approximate; this is not manufacturing CAD.

Each drawing's marking example is for IRF530S, not the represented device. The rendered IRF3710S, IRF5210S and IRF4905S text is illustrative; exact physical marking, typography, logo and production codes remain unverified.
