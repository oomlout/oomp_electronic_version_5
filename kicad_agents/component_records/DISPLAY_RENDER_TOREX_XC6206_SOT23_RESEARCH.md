# Torex XC6206 SOT-23 research

Three catalogue records identify Torex XC6206 SOT-23 regulators: `XC6206P332MR-G`, `XC6206P332MR` and `XC6206P502MR`. Their precise suffixes and existing identities are preserved. The first lists a 2.9 × 1.3 × 1.0 mm body; the latter two list 2.92 × 2.8 mm without an explicit body-versus-lead reference. All three select the KiCad SOT-23 footprint.

Torex's [XC6206 data sheet](https://product.torexsemi.com/system/files/series/xc6206.pdf) states that `-G` identifies a halogen- and antimony-free, RoHS-compliant option. Its SOT-23 marking rule specifies `6` for the series, `6` for the 3.1–6.0 V range and a third character of `2` for 3.3 V or `M` for 5.0 V; a fourth character is variable production-lot information. `662` and `66M` can therefore be used as partial fixed markings, omitting the variable lot. Font, placement and physical production marking remain illustrative. Preserve separate source entries and do not invent production-lot characters.

The inspected KiCad `Package_TO_SOT_SMD.3dshapes/SOT-23.step` has SHA-256 `dd5d1711204e1d8d26cd11490923bd84340820baa69538c385a4300ec2539bc0`, expected material colours and total bounds 2.5 × 3.0 × 1.2 mm. The `-G` record explicitly cites TO-236AB. Torex's [TO-236 package drawing](https://product.torexsemi.com/files/packages/sot-23-to-236-pkg.pdf) gives 2.4 ± 0.15 mm lead span, 2.9 ± 0.1 mm length and 1.15 mm maximum total height. For this exact entry, a vertical display scaling to 1.1 mm puts the model height within the drawing; 2.5 mm lead span and 3.0 mm length are within the stated ranges. Disclose this scaling. The two unsuffixed records need separate package review before assuming that their 2.8 mm width has the same reference. This is not a verified mechanical model or footprint.
