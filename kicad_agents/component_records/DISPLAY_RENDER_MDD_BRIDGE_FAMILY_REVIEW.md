# MDD bridge rectifier drawing review

The local MDD first-page PDFs below were visually inspected on 2026-11-21. Poppler reported font-substitution warnings; the package views, dimensions and marking rows were legible. These findings are review notes, not authority to change catalogue identities or footprints.

* `electronic_diode_bridge_rectifier_dip_4_mdd_microdiode_semiconductor_w06/datasheet.pdf`, SHA-256 `39c486b3432cda04fabd04d8c68438b23d83355137e09b9e2d9c464c72dc05ec`, is an MDD W005–W10 family document with W06 in the marking row. Its WOM drawing shows a **round body** approximately 8.6–9.1 mm across, 5–6 mm high, with four parallel 0.70–0.80 mm diameter leads in a square on 4.6–5.6 mm centres. The + lead projects at least 21 mm, the remaining three at least 18 mm. The bottom view has + and − on opposite corners, with the AC terminals on the other two. The catalogue W06 entry selects the rectangular `Diode_Bridge_DIP-4_W7.62mm_P5.08mm` footprint and lists 8.6 × 6.5 × 3.6 mm. Those should not be presented as an exact match to the drawing. No KiCad `WOM` or `W06` model was found by filename; a reviewed cylindrical model may show the drawing with the long positive lead and pin layout, while explicitly disclosing that top-face text/polarity placements are illustrative and that the source footprint and dimensions differ. The W06 PDF is not evidence for a different 2W10 orderable without independent corroboration.
* `electronic_diode_bridge_rectifier_kbl_mdd_microdiode_semiconductor_kbl406/datasheet.pdf`, SHA-256 `573c5f78e440cb38f2fe43f7cbd5bd394fbe38f1fd1a52dc3dd584afd5ff590f`, covers KBL4005–KBL410 and shows `MDD KBL406` and `MDD KBL410` in the marking row. The front outline is about 18.5–19.5 mm wide with a bevel and four leads; the drawing calls out +, AC, AC, − at the corresponding connections. The KBL410 entry has no local PDF. The source KBL406 dimensions 28.6 × 7.3 × 4.0 mm do not directly match the drawn body envelope and require explanation.
* `electronic_diode_bridge_rectifier_kbu_mdd_microdiode_semiconductor_kbu808/datasheet.pdf`, SHA-256 `71c9e8b3a1c01d94819527fcd24b98d44369d0b5e3972f2432a2434d1e41dc8b`, shows KBU808 in the marking row, a 22.7–23.7 mm wide body with a slotted mounting hole, bevel and four leads. The front view marks −, AC, AC, + from left to right, whereas the bottom diagram presents +, AC, AC, − in its own viewing orientation. Establish the viewing direction and actual KiCad terminal mapping before placing marks; do not simply copy the KBL/KBP overlay. Its catalogue dimensions also differ from the drawing's body width.

KiCad 10 supplies `Diode_Bridge_Vishay_KBL.step`, `Diode_Bridge_Vishay_KBU.step` and the selected rectangular DIP model. Their names alone do not prove material roles, manufacturer dimensions or terminal mapping. Inspect measured geometry and source face colours and disclose any differences before staging. Typeface and partial logo-free product text are illustrative; catalogue dimension or footprint conflicts must remain explicit in resulting pages.
