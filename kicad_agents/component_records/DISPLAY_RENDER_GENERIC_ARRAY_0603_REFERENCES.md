# UNI-ROYAL visual references for unsourced 4×0603 resistor arrays

The generic entries describe four 0603-size resistors and eight terminals, with
nominal resistance but no selected supplier or tolerance. Images illustrate the
named 5% UNI-ROYAL examples without assigning their supplier or tolerance to the
entries. KiCad geometry, materials, typeface and ink placement are illustrative.

The existing local UNI-ROYAL array specification JLC-01-015 documents the
4D03 convex 4-resistor circuit and nominal 3.20 × 1.60 × 0.50 mm dimensions
on pages 4–5, and three-digit 5% markings on page 8:
`parts_source/electronic_resistor_array_4_x_0402_convex_47000_ohm_8_pin/datasheet.pdf`
