# Xucheng XC-7 fuseholder: display model needs more evidence

The source identifies a Xucheng 5 × 20 BLX-A XC-7 bracket fuseholder. The
[LCSC C3131 listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/Fuseholders_Xucheng-Elec-5x20-BLX-A-XC-7_C3131.html)
confirms 5.2 × 20 mm fuse compatibility, 6.3 A, 250 V and one circuit. The
fuse size does not specify the holder's dimensions, contact layout or cover.
The source's 10 × 5 mm dimensions are generic, no local PDF or footprint is
attached, and no exact XC-7 model was identified in the installed KiCad fuse
library. Its available complete holder models are different manufacturer's parts.

An [EasyEDA component page](https://easyeda.com/component/5x20_BLX_A_XC_7-7504c0affcfc453c8609e5778cebfcf8)
identifies this holder but was inaccessible to the read-only web tool during
this review. Investigate whether it contains a usable, licensed 3D model or
obtain the exact mechanical drawing before staging a display. No PDF was
downloaded, and no generic holder has been substituted as an exact match.
