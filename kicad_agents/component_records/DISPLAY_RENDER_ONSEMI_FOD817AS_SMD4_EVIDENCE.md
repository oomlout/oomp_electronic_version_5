# onsemi FOD817AS SMD-4 display evidence

[onsemi's FOD814/FOD817 datasheet](https://www.onsemi.com/download/data-sheet/pdf/fod814-d.pdf) identifies the four-pin gull-wing CASE 709AH, 6.5 × 4.6 mm nominal body, 3.85 mm typical height, 10.16 mm nominal lead span and 2.54 mm pitch. The family marking diagram specifies 817 as the device code; logo, date, lot and other markings are omitted and typography and placement are illustrative. [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/onsemi/FOD817AS/1049600) identifies the exact FOD817AS orderable as surface-mount gull-wing.

The catalogue source gives 10 × 5 mm without dimension reference, height or footprint. Procedural display geometry follows nominal dimensions; individual lead profile, chamfers and pin-one dot are approximate. This is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
