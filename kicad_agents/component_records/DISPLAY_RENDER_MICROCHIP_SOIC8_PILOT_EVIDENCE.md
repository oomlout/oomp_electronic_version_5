# Microchip narrow SOIC-8 EEPROM display evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. Both exact catalogue parts are industrial temperature,
8-lead narrow SOIC and tape-and-reel variants. The manufacturer examples show
the first marking line listed below. The display deliberately shows that first
line only: the logo, package code, date, traceability and other lines are not
represented; typography and placement are illustrative, and markings may vary.
The catalogue records omit height. Microchip's C04-057 narrow SOIC drawing
specifies 1.27 mm pitch, approximately 4.9 × 3.9 mm body and 1.75 mm maximum
height, matching the reviewed KiCad nominal SOIC-8 model (6.0 × 4.9 × 1.75 mm
overall). No PDF was downloaded.
