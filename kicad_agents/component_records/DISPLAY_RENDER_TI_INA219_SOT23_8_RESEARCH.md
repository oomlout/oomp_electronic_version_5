# TI INA219 SOT-23-8 family research

The catalogue's generic INA219 entry implies Texas Instruments from its ID but omits manufacturer and exact orderable fields. Its dimensions are 2.9 × 1.6 × 1.1 mm; its selected KiCad footprint is instead VSSOP-8 2.3 × 2 mm, 0.5 mm pitch. Its separate package drawing also shows a 4.2 × 3.2 mm envelope and 0.4 mm pitch.

TI's [INA219 product page](https://www.ti.com/product/INA219/part-details/INA219AIDCNR) identifies INA219AIDCNR as an eight-contact SOT-23 (DCN) part; TI's [package list](https://www.ti.com/packaging/docs/searchtipackages.tsp?packageName=SO) gives DCN nominal 2.9 × 1.63 × 1.1 mm body and 0.65 mm pitch. TI's [data sheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ina219.pdf) lists INA219AIDCNR marking `A219`; this can represent that family variant, but the generic record does not identify an exact orderable. Other variants may have different markings. Do not invent lot/date/logo or claim the source VSSOP footprint is verified for the SOT-23 device.
