# TyoHM RN1WS 1.8 kΩ display evidence

The selected TyoHM RN1WS1.8KΩFT/BA1 / C385265 is listed as 1.8 kΩ, 1%, 1 W with a 9.2 × 3.3 mm body in the source's [LCSC listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C385265.html). The [TyoHM RN specification](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/lcsc/2304140030_TyoHM-RN1-2WS47KRFT-BA1_C385515.pdf) describes the colour-code convention. The 1% five-band code is brown, grey, black, brown, brown.

The KiCad DIN0309 model has a 9 × 3.2 mm body and formed leads at 12.70 mm, approximating the selected part. The existing quarter-watt ID and smaller 7.62 mm footprint/dimensions require separate catalogue review. Beige coating and band spacing are illustrative. The specification was read online; no local PDF is claimed.
