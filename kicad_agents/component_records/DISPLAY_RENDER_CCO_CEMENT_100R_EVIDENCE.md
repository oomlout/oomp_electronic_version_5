# CCO 100 Ω 5 W cement resistor display

The selected [LCSC C216402](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C216402.html)
listing, reviewed online on 2026-11-18, identifies CCO CR-L5W100Ω 5% P:
100 Ω, ±5%, 5 W, 22 × 9.5 mm body. The previously stored MPN was truncated
to `P`. The legacy quarter-watt ID, dimensions and footprint are incompatible
with this selected part and require review.

The KiCad R_Axial_Power_L25.0mm_W9.0mm_P27.94mm model is 25 × 9 mm, 3 mm
longer and 0.5 mm narrower than the listed body; its formed-lead pitch is
27.94 mm. It is a display approximation, not a footprint recommendation.
The plain `100Ω 5W` text communicates verified electrical values, but the
exact factory marking, font, layout and body finish were not verified and are
illustrative. No manufacturer PDF was saved locally in this review.
