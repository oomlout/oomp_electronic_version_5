# Toshiba TLP250(F) DIP8 display evidence

The catalogue records TOSHIBA TLP250(F). Toshiba's [TLP250 datasheet](https://media.digikey.com/pdf/Data%20Sheets/Toshiba%20PDFs/TLP250.pdf) shows an eight-lead DIP body nominally 9.66 × 6.4 mm with 7.62 mm lead pitch. An [exact-orderable distributor listing](https://www.octopart.in/isolators/toshiba-semiconductor/tlp250-f-/) describes TLP250(F) as 0.300 inch (7.62 mm) DIP. Toshiba's [photocoupler catalogue](https://toshiba.semicon-storage.com/info/TLP250_catalog_en_20200401_ALQ00217.pdf?did=61170&prodName=TLP250) describes an F lead form for some devices with a 10.16 mm span; we have not verified the meaning of the parenthesized (F) suffix with Toshiba. Therefore this is an illustrative standard-pitch representation and the actual lead form remains unconfirmed.

The render uses TLP250 as an illustrative family identifier. Exact physical marking, placement, orientation, manufacturer logo and production codes are unverified. The catalogue source gives 10 × 5 mm without specifying reference, height or footprint. The generic KiCad model has overall height 6.98 mm including pins and a generic notch; actual geometry and pin-one marking may differ. This is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
