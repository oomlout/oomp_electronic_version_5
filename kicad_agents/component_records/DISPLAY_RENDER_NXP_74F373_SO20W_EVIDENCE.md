# 74F373 SO20 display evidence

The Philips Semiconductors [74F373/374 datasheet hosted by Nexperia](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74F373_374.pdf), ordering table, lists N74F373D in 20-lead SOT163-1; the catalogue instead records 74F373D and manufacturer NXP Semicon. The datasheet SOT163-1 drawing specifies a 7.5 mm wide body and 1.27 mm pitch; catalogue lists 12.8 × 7.5 × 2.65 mm and SOIC-20W footprint. The generic KiCad model is scaled from 2.75 to 2.65 mm height, a display approximation, not a verified mechanical model.

The physical top marking for this catalogue orderable is not established; `74F373` is an illustrative family identifier. Prefix, suffix, logo, date and lot codes are omitted; typeface and placement are illustrative.
