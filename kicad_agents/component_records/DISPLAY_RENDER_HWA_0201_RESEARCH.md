# HWA CHN 0201 identity research

Two catalogue records contained the truncated manufacturer part number `CNTO`.
The LCSC product listings reviewed online give the full numbers, with the
spacing as displayed by LCSC:

| Catalogue ID | LCSC | Full manufacturer part number | Listed value / tolerance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `electronic_resistor_0201_270_ohm` | [C55098252](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C55098252.html) | `HRC0201J271 CNTO` | 270 Ω / 5% |
| `electronic_resistor_0201_390000_ohm` | [C55098269](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C55098269.html) | `HRC0201J394 CNTO` | 390 kΩ / 5% |

These links establish product identities and supplier specifications; they do
not establish the body-marking policy. Obtain and inspect HWA's own 0201
marking evidence before portraying either selected part. For an explicitly
labelled visual reference, Yageo RC0201 5% examples are documented unmarked;
do not claim that their appearance proves HWA's appearance or full electrical
interchangeability. No local manufacturer PDF is claimed here.

For later work: GiantOhm's GR series specification
https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/c3fe41080cc311a09e2390c2e42d8ed5.pdf?productCode=C49654531
documents GR0201 dimensions and ratings but was not found to state its 0201
marking policy. Yageo `RC0201JR-07680KL` is a documented 680 kΩ, 5% 0201
visual reference: https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/yageo/RC0201JR-07680KL/5280779
