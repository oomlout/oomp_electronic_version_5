# YXC 3225 12 MHz crystal display evidence

The source record identifies YXC X322512MSB4SI, 12 MHz, 20 pF and a 3.2 × 2.5 × 0.7 mm four-pad package. Its local `datasheet.pdf`, SHA-256 `7bc18549e407d8e381075b0f5fc696e48cf0fa060aeee67971925f43cfdfbb8b`, was visually inspected. It is a YXC YSX321SL family drawing: 3.20 ± 0.1 × 2.50 ± 0.1 × 0.7 ± 0.1 mm, four underside pads and a metal cap. It covers 8–64 MHz and 12 MHz at 80 ohm maximum ESR, but does not list the exact orderable number or its actual printed inscription. The small photograph illustrates the family, not a verified 12 MHz marking. Frequency text and its typography are illustrative.
