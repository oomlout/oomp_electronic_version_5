# MaxLinear SPX5205 SOT-23-5 historical marking evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. MaxLinear's [SPX5205 package page](https://www.maxlinear.com/product/power-management/power-conversion/ldos-and-regulators/linear-regulators-ldos/spx5205) identifies both exact M5 orderables as SOT-23-5 and lists 2.90 × 1.60 × 1.45 mm with 0.95 mm pitch. Local records list 2.9 × 1.6 mm and no height. The KiCad SOT-23-5 model is 1.55 mm high and its display-only height correction scales it to 1.45 mm, matching the listed thickness. This is an illustrative generic model, not a verified mechanical drawing.

MaxLinear's [2014 change notification](https://www.maxlinear.com/File?filePath=%2Ffiles%2Fchangenotifications%2FPCN_14-0727-03-1033.pdf) explicitly identifies `R33` and `R50` as previous package markings for SPX5205M5-L-3-3/TR and SPX5205M5-L-5-0/TR, respectively; replacement markings use `RCWW` and `SCWW`, where production-dependent fields are not reproduced here. The rendered markings are historical manufacturer examples, not a claim of the present marking. Typeface, placement, pin-one feature and omitted date/lot codes are illustrative.
