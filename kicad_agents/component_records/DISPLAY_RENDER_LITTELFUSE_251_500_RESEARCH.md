# Littelfuse 0251.500NRT1L display research

The source identifies Littelfuse 0251.500NRT1L, 500 mA, but has no local
datasheet or footprint. The [manufacturer's 251 series datasheet](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/littelfuse-fuse-251-253-datasheet?assetguid=f47a0bb7-8ede-4679-9646-7114c3787688)
was viewed online without downloading. Its table identifies 251.500 as 0.500 A
and 125 V. Its drawing gives a 7.11 mm long body, 2.80 mm maximum diameter,
and 0.64 mm wire; the illustration shows a green axial fuse with yellow ink.
The catalogue's generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions and `glass_plugin` taxonomy do
not describe that molded axial package. Preserve those fields and disclose the
difference in its display page.

The drawing illustrates 1 A and 5 A markings but does not explicitly show the
500 mA inscription. `LF 0.5A` is therefore illustrative, as are its typeface,
colour and placement. Render an axial green molded body with metal leads;
shortened display leads, exact body contours and material shades are
approximations, not manufacturer CAD or a PCB footprint.
