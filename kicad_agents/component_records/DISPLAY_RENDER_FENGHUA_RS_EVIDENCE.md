# Fenghua RS-05 and RS-06 5% display evidence

Reviewed online: Fenghua general thick-film resistor approval sheet R-6.1:
https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/8d58778925076bf80f4c3e6ed51a56f5.pdf?productCode=C115306

The title lists both RC and RS 05/06 package families. Section 3 identifies
RS as the upgraded power series, 05 as 0805, 06 as 1206 and J as 5%
tolerance. The resistance-value and body-marking sections on pages 5–7 use
three characters for E24 values: two significant digits plus the multiplier,
or R as the decimal point (for example 1R8 denotes 1.8 ohms). Confirm each
primary part number, 5% rating, value and footprint independently. Where the
catalogue states 1% while its selected MPN is J/5%, stop for reconciliation;
do not silently convert the component's rated tolerance.

than a production-lot photograph. The manufacturer sheet was reviewed online;
no local PDF is claimed.
