# Yageo visual references for unsourced generic 0805 and 1206 entries

The generic entries specify resistance and package, with no selected supplier
or tolerance. Images illustrate the identified Yageo 1% RC_L part, without
assigning that supplier or tolerance to the catalogue entry. KiCad geometry,
materials, typeface and placement are illustrative.

Yageo's [RC_L manufacturer specification](https://yageogroup.com/content/datasheet/asset/file/PYU-RC_GROUP_51_ROHS_L)
states that 1% 0805 and 1206 parts use four digits: three significant digits
and an exponent. It was reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed.
