# YAGEO FMP100 15 kΩ display evidence

The [YAGEO FMP specification](https://yageogroup.com/content/datasheet/asset/file/YAGEO-FMP_DATASHEET) documents a 6.3 ±0.5 × 2.4 ±0.2 mm FMP100 body, 1 W rating, and four-band 5% code. The [selected FMP100JT-52-15K](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/yageo/FMP100JT-52-15K/9107403) is 15 kΩ, 5%, 1 W: brown, green, orange, gold.

The catalogue ID retains a legacy quarter-watt name even though the selected part is rated 1 W. The KiCad 6.3 × 2.5 mm body is within the manufacturer's tolerance; its bent leads and beige coating are illustrative. The documents were read online and no local PDF is claimed.
