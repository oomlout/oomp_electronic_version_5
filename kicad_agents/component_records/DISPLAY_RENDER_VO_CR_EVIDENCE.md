# VO CR quarter-watt display evidence

The manufacturer-authored [CR carbon film specification](https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/12216322.html),
reviewed online on 2026-11-17, calls for MIL/EIA standard colour coding and
describes the normal-size finish as khaki. Its 1/4 W body is 6.2 ± 1 mm long
and 2.7 ± 1 mm in diameter, compatible with the nominal 6.3 by 2.5 mm KiCad
model. Leads in the display model are bent to the selected 7.62 mm footprint.

The specification does not explicitly state the number of bands on each MPN.
The standard 5% four-band scheme (two significant digits, multiplier, gold
tolerance) is illustrated by the [YAGEO carbon film marking table](https://s3.amazonaws.com/snapeda/datasheet/CFR-12JB-52-1M_Yageo.pdf).
It is used as a standards-based illustration; the exact band count and layout
for the individual VO parts are not verified. The finish, colours and spacing
are illustrative. No local PDF is claimed. Validate the exact supplier choice,
value, rating and footprint before rendering.
