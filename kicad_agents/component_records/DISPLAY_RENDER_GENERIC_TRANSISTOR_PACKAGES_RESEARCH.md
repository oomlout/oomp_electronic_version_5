# Unmarked generic transistor package illustrations

Reviewed 2026-11-21. The following source records are generic taxonomy
entries with no manufacturer, manufacturer part number, or local datasheet:

* `electronic_transistor_sot_23_bipolar_npn`
* `electronic_transistor_sot_23_bipolar_pnp`
* `electronic_transistor_sot_23_mosfet_n_channel_enhancement_mode`

Each specifies SOT-23 and 2.9 x 2.6 mm catalogue dimensions. The local
manufacturer SOT-23 family drawing reviewed in
`DISPLAY_RENDER_JSCJ_SOT23_DIODES_RESEARCH.md` gives 2.80–3.00 mm body
length, 2.25–2.55 mm overall lead span and 0.90–1.15 mm height. KiCad's
SOT-23 model has 2.5 x 3.0 x 1.2 mm overall bounds, vertically scaled to
1.1 mm in the display scene. This is a generic visual approximation, not
evidence for an exact transistor or electrical characteristics.

The illustrations are deliberately unmarked because these records do not
identify a manufacturer or orderable. Real parts may carry varied markings.
Dimensions, finish and geometry are illustrative, not manufacturing CAD or
a PCB footprint. Each part is identified by its catalogue name in its README.
