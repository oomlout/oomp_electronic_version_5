# MDD ES2JB SMB display research

The local MDD ES2AB–ES2JB family datasheet SHA-256
`3f81111d69c355c8972019e971d12763200fe0429ca4c32ff89db5f86094cde8`
page 1 was visually reviewed. It identifies ES2JB as DO-214AA/SMB, with
4.05–4.70 mm body length, 3.30–3.95 mm body width, 2.00–2.45 mm height,
4.90–5.60 mm overall span and a cathode band. The marking-code row appears
blank, so ES2JB text is an illustrative identifier, not verified marking.

KiCad `D_SMB.step` SHA-256
`029466277d91e9f5492556be93e46611049e07115c7913880c547afa96058ed4`
measures 5.30 × 3.55 × 2.15 mm overall. The source record has 10 × 5 mm
placeholder dimensions and no footprint; these are preserved but conflict
with the drawing. The model is illustrative, not manufacturer CAD or a PCB
footprint. No PDF was downloaded.
