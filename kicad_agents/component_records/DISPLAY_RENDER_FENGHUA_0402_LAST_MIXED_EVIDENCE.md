# Fenghua 0402 remaining mixed display evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-18. Fenghua general series specification FH-2016-001,
reproduced at https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/5746704.html, gives nominal
0402 dimensions 1.00 × 0.50 × 0.50 mm and tolerance code C = ±0.25 pF.
Individual thicknesses remain unverified. Exact 50 V identities and values:
- 0402CG3R0C500NT, 3 pF, C0G, ±0.25 pF: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1564.html
- 0402CG4R0C500NT, 4 pF, C0G, ±0.25 pF: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1568.html
- 0402F104M500NT, 100 nF, Y5V, ±20%: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1581.html
- 0402F153M500NT, 15 nF, Y5V, ±20%: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1583.html

The first two source records lack electrical fields. Their ±0.25 pF tolerance
is decoded from the family ordering table, while values and dielectric were
checked on the supplier listings. Generic model and unmarked appearance are
illustrative. No PDF was downloaded.
