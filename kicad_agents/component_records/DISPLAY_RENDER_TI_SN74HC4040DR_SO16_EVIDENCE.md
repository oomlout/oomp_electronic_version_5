# TI SN74HC4040DR SO16 display evidence and catalogue conflict

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [TI exact orderable addendum](https://www.ti.com/ods/sysadd/oa/symlink/sn74hc4040_oa.pdf) identifies SN74HC4040DR as 16-pin SOIC D with marking HC4040. TI's [D0016A outline](https://www.ti.com/lit/ml/mpds432a/mpds432a.pdf) specifies 9.8–10.0 × 3.8–4.0 mm body, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.75 mm maximum height. Local source identifies SN74HC4040DR but has an SSOP-16 5.3 × 6.2 mm footprint and 1.98 mm height, inconsistent with TI's exact part. TI lists SN74HC4040DBR as SSOP; do not conflate it with DR. The display depicts the exact TI DR package and explicitly discloses the unresolved source footprint/dimension conflict. It is not a verified PCB footprint; marking typography and layout are illustrative.
