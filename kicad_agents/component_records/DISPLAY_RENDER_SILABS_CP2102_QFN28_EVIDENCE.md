# Silicon Labs CP2102-GMR QFN28 display evidence

[Silicon Labs' CP2102/9 datasheet](https://www.silabs.com/documents/public/data-sheets/CP2102-9.pdf) gives QFN28 body 5 × 5 mm, height 0.80–1.00 mm, pitch 0.5 mm, and 2.90–3.35 mm exposed pad. [DigiKey](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/silicon-labs/CP2102-GMR/2051863) identifies the exact orderable in QFN28. The catalogue has the matching KiCad QFN-28-1EP_5x5mm_P0.5mm_EP3.35x3.35mm footprint, 5 × 5 mm body and no height. The KiCad model has 5 × 5 × 0.92 mm bounds, within the stated package height range.

CP2102 is an illustrative family identifier; exact type marking, logo, date and lot codes are unverified or omitted. This is display geometry, not a verified mechanical model or footprint.
