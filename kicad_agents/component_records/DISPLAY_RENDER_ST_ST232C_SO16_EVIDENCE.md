# ST ST232CDR SO-16 display evidence

ST's [datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/st232c.pdf), page 1, identifies ST232CDR in SO-16. Page 12 specifies 9.8–10.0 mm length, 3.8–4.0 mm body width, 5.8–6.2 mm lead span, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.75 mm maximum height. The catalogue lists 9.9 × 3.9 mm but omits height and footprint. The KiCad SOIC-16 display model is an approximation and not a verified footprint.

The datasheet does not establish the top marking for this orderable. `ST232C` is an illustrative family identifier, not a confirmed manufacturer marking; logo, lot/date codes, typeface, placement and orientation are illustrative or omitted. The related ST232EC is a different device and its marking cannot establish the ST232C marking.
