# UNI-ROYAL 2.7 MΩ and Stackpole 8.2 MΩ displays

Reviewed online 2026-11-18; existing supplier selections retained.

* [Tayda's MF0W4FF2704A50 listing](https://www.taydaelectronics.com/resistor-2-7m-ohm-1-4w-1-metal-film-pkg-of-10.html)
  confirms Royal Ohm's 2.7 MΩ, 250 mW, 1% metal-film part. The existing LCSC
  selection C2991790 records a 6.5 × 2.2 mm body; Tayda lists 6.8 × 2.5 mm.
* [Stackpole RC12JB8M20](https://www.nacsemi.com/products/stackpole/rc-carbon-composition-resistors/)
  is 8.2 MΩ, 500 mW, 5%. The selected LCSC C19511659 lists 9.5 × 3.6 mm.
  The legacy catalogue quarter-watt ID and DIN0207 footprint need review.

Standard value codes are red/violet/black/yellow/brown (1%) and
grey/red/green/gold (5%). Exact manufacturer stripe count, spacing and shades
are unverified. The beige body finish is illustrative. KiCad DIN0207 6.3 × 2.5
mm and DIN0309 9 × 3.2 mm models approximate the listed body sizes. The DIN0309
model has 12.70 mm formed-lead pitch and differs from the existing footprint.
No manufacturer PDF was saved locally in this review.
