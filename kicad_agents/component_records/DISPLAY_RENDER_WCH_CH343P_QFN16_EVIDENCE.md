# WCH CH343P QFN16 display evidence

[WCH's CH343 datasheet](https://cdn-learn.adafruit.com/assets/assets/000/134/549/original/CH343DS1.PDF?1737477957=) specifies CH343P as QFN16 with 3 × 3 mm body and 0.5 mm pitch. The catalogue has the CH343P part number but no manufacturer, so WCH is inferred from the orderable and catalogue ID. KiCad's generic QFN16 model has 3 × 3 × 0.77 mm bounds and 1.8 × 1.8 mm exposed pad. The catalogue does not specify height or footprint; the exact device's height and pad geometry are unverified. A [KiCad package-generator review](https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-library-tools/-/merge_requests/1350/reports) describes ambiguity in the height of the WCH variant.

CH343P is an illustrative identifier rather than a verified physical top marking; logo, typography, date and lot codes are omitted or illustrative. The model is for display and is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
