# HDGCPH-PZ01-21 21-position header display research

The reviewed source https://jlcpcb.com/parts/2nd/Connectors/Pin_Headers_3127?Manufacturer=HDGC identifies HDGCPH-PZ01-21 as a black, straight
1×21 2.54 mm header with 2.54 mm listed body, 6 mm mating pins and 3 mm tails.
The listing identifies gold-plated brass; the gold shade is illustrative. The local generated PDF is a different manufacturer Cankemeng drawing and is not evidence for HDGC.

The KiCad model is generic display geometry, not manufacturer CAD or a PCB
footprint. The source's 2.48 mm width differs from KiCad's 2.54 mm. The
legacy model and render remain in the source folder. No PDF was downloaded.

KiCad model SHA-256 `b3f269f2ad16858a5ba40470c9c6adcd37320c0ab14a4f72b49a7caa5da5d699`, bounds 2.54 × 53.34 × 11.54 mm.
