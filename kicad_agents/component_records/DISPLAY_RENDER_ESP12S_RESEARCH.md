It has no manufacturer field or exact manufacturer part number; the ID says Espressif, but ESP-12S modules are commonly supplied by Ai-Thinker around Espressif silicon. Do not silently claim the source identifies Ai-Thinker as its manufacturer.

Ai-Thinker's indexed [ESP-12S specification](https://docs.ai-thinker.com/_media/esp-12s_specification_v1.0.pdf) identifies a 24 x 16 x 3 +/- 0.2 mm module; the indexed [series module manual](https://docs.ai-thinker.com/_media/esp8266/esp8266_series_modules_user_manual_en.pdf) lists 2 mm terminal pitch, 1 x 1.2 mm terminal features and 0.8 +/- 0.1 mm PCB thickness. These links redirected to the manufacturer's site landing page during this review, so the drawings and the exact shield marking have not yet been visually verified. No manufacturer PDF was downloaded. Search snippets alone do not establish a complete physical marking.

The source drawing metadata lists a 16-contact layout but its stored pin coordinates include bottom-edge features and four contacts along an edge, conflicting with its prose and the expected two rows of eight; investigate the manufacturer drawing before reproducing contacts. Local KiCad has an ESP-12E model, but that is not automatically an ESP-12S: inspect its pad counts, dimensions and antenna before deciding whether a guarded adaptation can accurately represent this record.
