# MDD 1N400x axial diode evidence

The existing MDD 1N4001 local PDF SHA-256 `ffae0d09201187997d60c8ad051b739b5ebc3db20a46b13fbceb860d1db8e22a`, page 1, is a 1N4001–1N4007 family sheet. Its marking-code row lists MDD with each identifier. Its A-405 package drawing shows a 4.20–5.20 mm long cylindrical body, 2.30–2.70 mm body diameter, 0.55–0.65 mm lead diameter, at least 25.4 mm lead length per side and a polarity band. Source entries 1N4001 and 1N4004 identify MDD and select a KiCad DO-41/SOD81 horizontal footprint; 1N4004 has no local PDF. `1N4007G` is not an exact orderable in this family sheet, so its suffix needs independent evidence before attributing it to the sheet.

The catalogue says DO-41 while the local drawing says A-405; dimensions and physical form should be compared carefully and the distinction disclosed. The KiCad model `D_DO-41_SOD81_P10.16mm_Horizontal.step` SHA-256 `c3e23d4b276811b74eecb329bd4480209ad3dbc8cf6e6e3f2a5b542c5ca343c1` has bent leads, unlike the straight-lead manufacturer drawing. Before staging, inspect the model's cylinder dimensions, polarity band placement and an actual marked preview. A partial identifier without the MDD logo can be used only with typography, geometry and production details described as illustrative. Do not modify the selected footprint as part of display work.

Additional source: an [MDD-authored 1N4001G–1N4007G family sheet](https://xonstorage.blob.core.windows.net/pdf/microdiodeelectronics_1n4007_apr22_xonlink.pdf) explicitly lists 1N4007G, marking row, DO-41 and the same 4.20–5.20 mm body length, 2.30–2.70 mm body diameter and 0.55–0.65 mm lead diameter. The G-suffix record has no local PDF. This resolves the earlier exact-suffix gap for an online manufacturer-authored source; no PDF was downloaded.

The installed KiCad model was inspected by geometry group: its cylindrical body is 5.20 mm long and about 2.70 mm in diameter, at the drawing maxima. Its bent lead diameter is about 0.80 mm, **0.15 mm larger** than the drawing maximum. The light cathode band is nearer the negative-X end, matching pin 1 of the chosen footprint; the exact stripe width is not established. Overall bent-model bounds are approximately 10.96 x 2.72 x 5.71 mm. The 10.16 mm bend pitch is a display/footprint configuration, not the straight 25.4 mm minimum lead length in the source drawings. Both the lead diameter discrepancy and bent configuration must be disclosed.
