# TI SN74HC244N DIP-20 display pilot research

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [TI SN74HC244 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hc244.pdf) identifies exact SN74HC244N as 20-pin PDIP N with marking `SN74HC244N`. Its 2026 package-information table lists nominal N-package length 24.33 mm and width 6.35 mm; local source records 25.7 × 6.35 × 3.3 mm and DIP-20 W7.62 mm. This length discrepancy needs explicit disclosure, and the manufacturer and model should be checked against the actual drawing before rendering.

The installed KiCad `Package_DIP.3dshapes/DIP-20_W7.62mm.step` SHA-256 `ca359c9b9e0ce94efcb6c96866513f659eef6517d629ace3c4a0a779f6fd57bb` has measured full bounds 7.874 × 26.162 × 6.980 mm, including 3.3 mm of projecting pins. The model is longer than TI's nominal body: do not describe it as exact, use display-only appropriate correction only if justified and disclosed, or clearly label approximation if retained. Two STEP face colors separate body and metallic leads; a moulded notch marks the pin-one end. Review 20 leads, notch, text and framing in a preview before generating a final or publishing. Typeface, placement and variable manufacturer/date/lot codes are illustrative.

Further locally present exact N-orderables include SN74HC245N and SN74HC374N with explicit manufacturer-listed marks in their individual TI data sheets; verify each drawing and local source before candidate staging. SN74HC273N, SN74HC373N and SN74HC573AN need individual marking evidence.

Follow-up: TI's related [SN74AHCT374 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74ahct374.pdf) describes the 20-pin N package as 25.4 mm nominal body while its own table also shows 24.33 mm package size, a conflicting internal relationship. Manufacturer information is therefore not yet reconciled; do not infer that a uniform scale is warranted. Review an N 20-pin mechanical drawing before choosing model or correction. The KiCad full length and source length are similar, but do not assume manufacturer compliance from that alone.

Clarification: TI’s [product overview](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74HC244) separately gives 24.33 × 9.4 mm as the N-package footprint summary. The 9.4 mm is not the 6.35 mm body-width figure; the source does not establish that all these reported sizes measure the same feature. TI’s [N0020A drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/ml/mpdi096/mpdi096.pdf) shows a 1.013–1.040 inch length (25.73–26.42 mm), but its applicability to this exact orderable has not been established. Do not assert that it resolves the mismatch.
