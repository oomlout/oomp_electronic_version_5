# Bourns MF-FSMF family display research

The source identifies the MF-FSMF 0603 family, but deliberately does not select
a current suffix. The manufacturer family datasheet was viewed online, not
downloaded: https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/mf-fsmf.pdf . Its
dimensions table lists length 1.45–1.85 mm, width 0.65–1.05 mm, and thickness
0.30–0.65 mm for 010X–035X or 0.65–1.00 mm for 050X. It illustrates
concave ends and specifies nickel/gold plating. A 1.6 × 0.8 × 0.65 mm display
is compatible with the common boundaries but does not identify a variant.

The family uses five different markings (II, I, +, dot, and dash) depending
on model. Since the source does not give a suffix, omit the marking; this is
a deliberately unmarked illustration, not a claim that real parts are blank.
Body shade, exact notch shape and finish are illustrative, not manufacturer
CAD or a PCB footprint. A scaled existing procedural concave-end model is an
approximation and the manufacturer drawing is the authority.
