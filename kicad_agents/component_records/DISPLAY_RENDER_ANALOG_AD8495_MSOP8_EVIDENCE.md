# Analog Devices AD8495ARMZ MSOP-8 display evidence

The [Analog Devices datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ad8494_8495_8496_8497.pdf) identifies AD8495ARMZ as MSOP-8 and lists Y33 as its marking code. The catalogue ID incorrectly contains `texas_instruments`, while its manufacturer and MPN fields are empty; AD8495ARMZ is an Analog Devices example inferred from the ID, and the manufacturer discrepancy is unresolved in source taxonomy. Do not infer that Texas Instruments manufactures this device.

The catalogue lists 3 × 3 × 1.1 mm and an MSOP-8 footprint. The generic KiCad model has 4.9 × 3 × 0.95 mm overall bounds and is an illustrative display approximation, not a manufacturer-qualified footprint. Y33 is manufacturer-listed for AD8495ARMZ; typography, placement, date/lot and logo are illustrative or omitted.
