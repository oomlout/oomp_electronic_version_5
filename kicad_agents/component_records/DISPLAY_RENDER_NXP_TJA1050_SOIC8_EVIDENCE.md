# NXP TJA1050T/CM,118 SO8 display evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [NXP product declaration](https://www.nxp.com/webapp/chemical-content/TJA1050T_CM_cm_118.html) identifies the exact orderable as SO8 SOT96; [SOT96-1 package information](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/package-information/SOT96-1.pdf) gives 4.9 × 3.9 mm nominal body, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.75 mm maximum seated height. The catalogue source omits height. An [NXP technical support response](https://community.nxp.com/t5/Other-NXP-Products/Top-side-marking-information-MPN-TJA1050T-CM-118/td-p/749356) confirms that a customer-reported `A1050/C` format relates to a wafer-fab transfer, with an earlier reported `TJA1050` marking. The render illustrates the reported A1050/C variant; manufacturer/date/lot codes are omitted and typography and layout are illustrative. It does not establish every manufacturing variant.
