# Chengx KS 4 × 7 mm radial display candidates

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [JLCPCB C2760](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/3141-KS105M050C07RR0VH2FP0/C2760)
identifies KS105M050C07RR0VH2FP0 as 1 µF / 50 V and
[JLCPCB C2065](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/2417-KS225M050C07RR0VH2FP0/C2065)
identifies KS225M050C07RR0VH2FP0 as 2.2 µF / 50 V. Both listings specify
polarized 4 × 7 mm cans with 1.5 mm lead spacing. A C2760 datasheet exists
in its source directory; no C2065 PDF was captured locally. The linked JLCPCB
PDF endpoints could not be opened by the research tool during this review.

The nearby [manufacturer KS336 specification](https://doc.chipmall.com/datasheet/rev_2501081354_cx-dongguan-chengxing-elec-ks336m016c07rr0vh2fp0_c80919328.pdf)
describes another 4 × 7 mm KS-series part with black PVC and white print,
and its outline specifies vents only over 6.3 mm diameter. Using its unvented
top and black/white appearance for these siblings is an illustrative inference,
not proof of their individual production artwork. The value, voltage and KS
series text, stripe, typography, proportions, top and finish remain illustrative.
Inspect each preview and polarity before publication. No PDF was downloaded.
