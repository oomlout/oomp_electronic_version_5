# Winbond W25Q16JVUXIQ USON-8 display research

Winbond's [selection guide](https://www.winbond.com/export/sites/winbond/product-selection-guide/file/2025-Product-Selection-Guide-Winbond-Code-Storage-Flash-Memory.pdf) identifies exact `W25Q16JVUXIQ` as USON-8, 2 × 3 mm. A [Winbond-authored W25Q16JV data sheet](https://robu-prod-media.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2025/02/Winbond-W25Q16JVSSIQ-datasheet.pdf) hosted by Robu describes UX nominal body 3.00 × 2.00 × 0.55 mm, eight terminals in two rows and 0.5 mm pitch. The source record's package drawing places two terminals on each of four sides and selects a 2 × 2 mm footprint; both conflict with this drawing. Preserve the source and disclose these discrepancies.

The same data sheet's top-marking table identifies a compact `1P` prefix followed by variable date and lot characters for UX; do not invent those variable characters. Type, size and placement remain illustrative. The part identity appears in the catalogue ID, but the source has no populated manufacturer or manufacturer-part-number fields, so the identification is inferred and must be disclosed.

The KiCad candidate `Package_DFN_QFN.3dshapes/DFN-8-1EP_2x3mm_P0.5mm_EP0.61x2.2mm.step` is valid, with expected coating, termination and pin-indicator colours, SHA-256 `d1a0c43ea32a8b2ce1b2e57208cef09b38462bda190e1253857c35649bfc56f7` and overall bounds 2.0 × 2.98 × 0.77 mm. Its height exceeds Winbond's 0.60 mm maximum; a display candidate would need to be scaled vertically to 0.55 mm and disclose the adjustment. It includes a 0.61 × 2.2 mm exposed pad, whereas the extracted UX drawing does not clearly establish a centre pad. Do not claim an exact match without visually checking the maker drawing and pin layout. Any candidate needs visual review of terminals and pin-one and must disclose the unresolved pad difference. Not a verified footprint or mechanical model.
