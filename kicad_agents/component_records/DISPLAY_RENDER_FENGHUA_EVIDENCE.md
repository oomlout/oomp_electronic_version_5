# Fenghua RC general resistor display evidence

Manufacturer-authored RC/RS general chip resistor approval sheet R-6.1,
reviewed online at:
https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/8d58778925076bf80f4c3e6ed51a56f5.pdf?productCode=C115306

- Section 2.2 gives 0201 nominal 0.60 × 0.30 × 0.23 mm and 0402 nominal
  1.00 × 0.50 × 0.30 mm, with 0402 height tolerance ±0.10 mm. KiCad's 0402
  nominal height is 0.35 mm, within that tolerance, and is illustrative.
- Section 3 gives the RC part-number fields and value notation. The resistance
  marking section says 01005, 0201 and 0402 glass sides have no marking.
- Appendix 1 lists resistance ranges and temperature coefficients. Check the
  exact primary part number, manufacturer, footprint and value independently.

The document was inspected online; no local PDF is claimed. The generic model,
materials and colour are illustrative and do not guarantee lot appearance.
Other series and ambiguous identifiers require separate research.
