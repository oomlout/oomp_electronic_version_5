# TI BQ24070RHLR VQFN research

The source record identifies Texas Instruments BQ24070RHLR, has no machine-solder footprint, and lists generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions. The [TI exact-orderable page](https://www.ti.com/product/BQ24070/part-details/BQ24070RHLR) specifies the 20-pin RHL VQFN with 3.5 × 4.5 mm body. TI's [package material and drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24070.pdf) give 0.5 mm pitch and 1 mm maximum height. Its [quality report](https://www.ti.com/quality-reliability-packaging-download/ko-kr/report?opn=BQ24070RHLR) gives fixed device marking `BRQ`; logo, layout and variable date/lot are not established here.

The installed KiCad `Package_DFN_QFN:Texas_VQFN-RHL-20` footprint has eight contacts on each long side, two numbered contacts on each end, and a central pad with additional extensions. Its referenced `Texas_VQFN-RHL-20_ThermalVias.step` is absent. The only installed 3.5 × 4.5 mm STEP candidate is a 28-pin Texas RVE model, so it is unsuitable. TI's RHL0020A package drawing (page 31 of the data sheet) gives 3.4–3.6 × 4.4–4.6 mm body, 0.5 mm pitch, 20 signal contacts, and a 2.05 ± 0.1 × 3.05 ± 0.1 mm central pad. The drawing's pin numbering confirms the eight-plus-two layout.

`kicad_agents/display_model_ti_rhl0020a.py` builds a nominal 3.5 × 4.5 × 0.9 mm display body, 20 signal contacts, central pad with approximate extensions, and an illustrative pin-one marker. The 0.04 mm terminal reveal, pad shape and marker are display approximations; do not use as a PCB footprint. Disclose the generic source dimension conflict and unverified logo/date/lot. Model geometry and material groups were checked; terminal reveals and marking require visual review.
