# Honor RVT 6.3 × 5.4 mm display candidate

The [Honor RVT specification](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf)
pages 4–6 calls for value, voltage and negative polarity and lists this case.
[LCSC C3344](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3344.html) identifies
RVT1V470M0605 as 47 µF, 35 V, 6.3 × 5.4 mm. The KiCad model's dark sector
is on negative pad 2. Numeric text, finish and sector shape are illustrative;
exact ink, maker logo and date are unverified. No PDF was downloaded.
