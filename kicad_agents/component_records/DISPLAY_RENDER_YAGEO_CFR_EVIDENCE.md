# YAGEO CFR-25 carbon-film resistor display evidence

Reviewed online: YAGEO CFR carbon-film resistor product specification,
V.0, 13 August 2021, manufacturer-authored PDF hosted by SnapEDA:
https://s3.amazonaws.com/snapeda/datasheet/CFR-12JB-52-1M_Yageo.pdf

The CFR-25 nominal body is 6.3 mm long and 2.4 mm in diameter. Page 14
shows the four-band colour code used for 5% parts: two significant digits,
a multiplier and a gold tolerance band. The catalogue record for
`CFR-25JB-52-6K8` specifies 6.8 kΩ, 5%, 250 mW; its four bands are blue,
grey, red and gold. Verify the primary manufacturer, exact MPN, value,
tolerance and footprint for any other part before extending this rule.

The KiCad model uses leads bent to a 7.62 mm footprint. Traditional beige
body colour, individual band shades and spacing, and the bent-lead presentation
are illustrative, not a production-lot photograph. The document was reviewed
online; no local manufacturer PDF has been captured.
