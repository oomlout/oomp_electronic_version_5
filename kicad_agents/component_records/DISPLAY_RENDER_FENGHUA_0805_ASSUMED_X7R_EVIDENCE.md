# Fenghua 0805 X7R illustrations with assumed thickness

Reviewed 2026-11-18. The Fenghua general series specification reproduced at
https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/5746704.html lists 0805 length 2.00 mm, width
1.25 mm and multiple thicknesses (0.8, 1.0 and 1.25 mm). The 0.8 mm
illustration is one plausible family size, not an independently verified
exact-part thickness. Each source record lacks height. The following 50 V
X7R ±10% selected parts were identified from individual supplier listings:

- 0805B154K500NT, 150 nF: https://www.compel.ru/infosheet/SAMSUNG/CL21B154KBFNNNG (comparison table)
- 0805B151K500NT, 150 pF: https://m.elecfans.com/article/xc-2500236135.html
- 0805B181K500NT, 180 pF: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1721.html
- 0805B183K500NT, 18 nF: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1723.html

Some Elecfans pages report 1.25 cm thickness; this is implausible for 0805
and is not treated as evidence for the exact-part thickness. Colour, finish and
blank appearance are illustrative. No PDF was downloaded.
