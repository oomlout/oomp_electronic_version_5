# CCO MF1/8W small-body display evidence

The selected [27 Ω / C119144](https://item.szlcsc.com/120413.html) and [680 kΩ / C119242](https://item.szlcsc.com/120511.html) are each CCO metal-film, 1%, 125 mW, with nominal 3.2 × 1.8 mm bodies. Their manufacturer numbers were previously truncated to `T` in the source; the linked individual supplier pages identify the complete numbers.

The [CCO metal-film specification](https://robu-prod-media.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2025/02/C119315.pdf) describes the family and five-band coding. Standard bands are red/violet/black/gold/brown for 27 Ω and blue/grey/black/orange/brown for 680 kΩ. Actual stripe spacing and shades are illustrative; CCO specifies sky-blue bodies and beige is the requested illustrative finish.

The KiCad 3.6 × 1.6 mm DIN0204 body approximates the selected parts, with formed leads on 7.62 mm centers. The existing quarter-watt IDs and larger DIN0207 footprint/dimensions require separate catalogue review. The specification was read online; no local PDF is claimed.
