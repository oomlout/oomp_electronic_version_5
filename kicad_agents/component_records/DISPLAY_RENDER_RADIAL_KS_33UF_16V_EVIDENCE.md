# Chengx KS 33 µF / 16 V radial display evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. The catalogue's KS336M016C07RR0VH2FP0 / C3314 is
identified as a 33 µF, 16 V polarized part with 4 × 7 mm body and 1.5 mm
lead spacing by [LCSC](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3314.html).
The [manufacturer's specification](https://doc.chipmall.com/datasheet/rev_2501081354_cx-dongguan-chengxing-elec-ks336m016c07rr0vh2fp0_c80919328.pdf)
identifies the same manufacturer part, a black PVC sleeve with white print,
and a drawing with 4 mm diameter, 7 mm height, 1.5 mm pitch and 0.45 mm
lead diameter. Its outline says a vent is present for diameters greater than
6.3 mm. The document was viewed online; no local PDF was captured.

The KiCad 4 mm radial model has a 4 mm high can and a cross vent. The planned
display adaptation lengthens only its straight sleeve to 7 mm, preserves the
ends and leads, and replaces the metal top groove with an unvented disk. The
manufacturer drawing is the source for omitting the vent, but the exact top
finish and depth are illustrative. The proposed sleeve text `33uF / 16V /
KS 105C`, stripe, typography and arrangement are illustrative: the drawing
does not establish production artwork. The negative stripe must correspond
to footprint pad 2 before publication. Geometry and finish are display-only,
not manufacturing data. Verify the completed model and preview before use.
