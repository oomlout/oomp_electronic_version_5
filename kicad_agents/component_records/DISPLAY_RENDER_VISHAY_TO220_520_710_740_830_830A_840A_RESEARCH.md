approximately 13.435 mm projection lies in that range. Its overall bounds are
10.25 x 4.455 x 28.525 mm. Dimensions and finish are approximate and the model
is not verified manufacturing CAD. The local family PDFs and drawings do not
establish the exact printed markings or independently confirm each PbF suffix;
the rendered device identifiers are illustrative, with typography, logo and
lot/date details unverified.
