# Xucheng 5F.0500210000R1 glass fuse display research

The [LCSC listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3123.html) identifies
Xucheng 5F.0500210000R1 as a 500 mA, 250 V, 5.2 × 20 mm glass fuse. No local
PDF is attached to this particular record. The existing 2 A sibling's Xucheng
5F PDF, `parts_source/electronic_fuse_5_2_x_20_mm_glass_plugin_disposable_xucheng_elec_5f_2000210000r1/datasheet.pdf`,
SHA-256 `eed9cc6991947ce18ab00afc8f4fb6e2f727c69e5cc3a9125ea6945fc8681681`,
was previously visually reviewed. It depicts a transparent tube, metal caps,
visible wire and identifies 5F as quick-blow. That sibling document does not
specify this 500 mA product or its exact cap inscription.

An independently rendered illustration may use the 5.2 × 20 mm glass model.
The end text `F500mA 250V` is illustrative based on family/rating information,
not a verified production marking. Cap length, wire size, glass wall thickness
and finishes are approximate. The source 6.5 mm height conflicts with the
nominal 5.2 mm cylindrical diameter. This is not manufacturer CAD or a PCB
footprint. No PDF was downloaded in this research.
