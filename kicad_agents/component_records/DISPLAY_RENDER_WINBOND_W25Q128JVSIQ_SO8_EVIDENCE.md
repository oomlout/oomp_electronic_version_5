# Winbond W25Q128JVSIQ SOIC-8 display evidence

Winbond's [W25Q128JV datasheet](https://community.nxp.com/pwmxy87654/attachments/pwmxy87654/lpc/43466/1/nor%2520flash1.pdf), sections 10.1 and 11, lists the exact W25Q128JVSIQ orderable with top-side type marking 25Q128JVSQ, 5.28 × 5.28 mm nominal SOIC-8 body, 7.90 mm nominal lead span, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.75–2.16 mm overall height. Logo, variable codes, typography and layout are illustrative or omitted.

The KiCad SOIC-8 model has a 5.3 × 5.3 mm nominal body, 7.8 mm lead span and 2.16 mm overall bounds. The catalogue records 5.28 × 5.23 mm without height or footprint. This is display geometry, not a verified mechanical model or footprint.
