# TI SOIC-14 additional logic evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. The exact orderable parts, SOIC (D) packages and markings
below are listed by TI in the linked individual datasheets. Each contains the
D0014A 14-pin outline: 8.55–8.75 mm body length, 3.8–4.0 mm body width,
5.8–6.2 mm typical lead span, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.75 mm maximum height. The
reviewed KiCad model has nominal overall bounds 6.0 × 8.7 × 1.75 mm. Catalogue
height must be distinguished from TI's maximum. Typeface, orientation,
placement and production codes are illustrative. No PDF was downloaded.

For SN74HC21QDRQ1 the first-page ordering table lists HC21Q, while the current
package addendum lists **both HC21Q and HC21Q1**. The display uses HC21Q as one
documented variant; real parts can carry either manufacturer marking.
