# ST STM32F103C8Tx LQFP48 display evidence

[ST's STM32F103x8 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32f103c8.pdf), section 6.9, specifies a 48-lead LQFP with 7 × 7 mm body, 9 × 9 mm nominal lead span, 0.5 mm pitch and 1.60 mm maximum height. The source specifies 9.7 × 9.7 mm without defining its reference and selects the KiCad LQFP-48_7x7mm_P0.5mm footprint; 9.7 mm resembles the recommended pad pattern span rather than the chip's nominal lead span. Source has no manufacturer or exact orderable; ST and the family are inferred from its ID, and the `x` in STM32F103C8Tx denotes an unspecified option. Do not represent a specific temperature grade as confirmed.

`STM32F103C8` is an illustrative family identifier; exact physical top marking, logo, date, lot and revision are unverified or omitted. Inspect the corresponding KiCad model bounds and colours before use. The display is not a verified mechanical model or footprint.
