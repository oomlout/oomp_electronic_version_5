# TI NS-20 batch 01 evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. Each TI ordering table identifies the exact orderable as 20-pin SOP NS and states its package code. The [TI NS package drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/MSOP002A), linked from the SN74HC374NSR exact orderable page and visually reviewed, gives 12.30–12.90 mm body length, 5.00–5.60 mm body width, 7.40–8.20 mm lead span, 1.27 mm pitch and 2.00 mm maximum height. All local source records specify 7.2 x 5.3 x 1.98 mm and a 0.65 mm SSOP footprint, which conflicts with the NS package. Preserve source data and disclose the mismatch. The generic KiCad SOIC-20W model is scaled 75% across width and from 2.75 to 2.00 mm high for display; estimated body width 5.625 mm is 0.025 mm above the drawing maximum. Not a verified mechanical model or footprint. Typeface, orientation, placement, pin-one indicator, logo and variable production codes are illustrative.
