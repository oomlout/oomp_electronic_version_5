# VO MF1W 1.5 kΩ display evidence

The selected [VO MF1W-1K5±1%-TT63 / C2903196](https://www.lcsc.com/fr/product-detail/C2903196.html) is a 1.5 kΩ, 1%, 1 W metal-film part with a listed 11 × 4.5 mm body. The [VO MF specification](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/056778fc22ae118b55bed9161ea6c1d8.pdf?productCode=C2903339) describes standard colour coding. Brown/green/black/brown/brown is a standard 1% five-band representation; exact band count and placement for this item are unverified.

The KiCad DIN0414 model has an 11.9 × 4.5 mm body and formed leads at 15.24 mm; it approximates the selected body. The existing quarter-watt ID and smaller 7.62 mm footprint/dimensions require separate catalogue review. Beige coating is illustrative. The specification was read online; no local PDF is claimed.
