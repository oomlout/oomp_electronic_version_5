# TAJ C-case display batch 01 evidence

The manufacturer [TAJ datasheet](https://datasheets.kyocera-avx.com/TAJ.pdf)
sets out the positive-anode stripe, value and voltage marking codes and C-case
nominal 6.0 × 3.2 × 2.6 mm envelope. KiCad Kemet C geometry is 2.8 mm
high and is scaled to nominal height for display. Markings, body finish,
logo and lot codes are illustrative; higher-rated voltage substitutions can
change the physical markings. Shared material-pass composites approximate
indirect reflections.
