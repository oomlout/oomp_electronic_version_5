# Yageo visual references for three selected small resistors

Selected-part appearance and marking policies are unverified. Each image names
the exact Yageo reference it illustrates. Equal nominal resistance, tolerance
and package do not establish complete interchangeability. KiCad geometry and
materials are illustrative. The 0402 10 MΩ record also lists alternative 5%
parts; this image illustrates a 1% example, not those alternatives.

The [Yageo RC_L specification](https://yageogroup.com/content/datasheet/asset/file/PYU-RC_GROUP_51_ROHS_L),
page 3, states that 0201 and 0402 bodies are unmarked. Manufacturer reference
specifications were reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed.
