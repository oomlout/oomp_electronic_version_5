text layouts, stripe, precise colour, proportions and finish are illustrative.
KiCad D16/P7.5 and D18/P7.5 models use straight-sleeve extensions of 9 mm and
12 mm, respectively, preserving ends and leads. Real-mesh checks, pad-2
negative stripe alignment and individual visual reviews are required before
publication. No PDF was downloaded.
