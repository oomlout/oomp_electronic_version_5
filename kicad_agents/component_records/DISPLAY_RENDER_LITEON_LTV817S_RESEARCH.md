# Lite-On LTV-817S-TA1-C optocoupler display research

The catalogue identifies Lite-On `LTV-817S-TA1-C` and records generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions with no verified machine-solder footprint. Its suffix must remain intact; C is a current-transfer-ratio rank, not a different body shape.

Lite-On's [LTV-8X7 series data sheet](https://optoelectronics.liteon.com/upload/download/DS-70-96-0016/LTV-8X7%20series%20%20Rev.T.PDF) identifies `LTV-817S` as the four-pin surface-mount form and `TA1` as a reel option. It gives C as a 200–400% CTR rank and says rank may or may not appear on the package. It also describes variable year, week and factory markings. An older [Lite-On drawing hosted by LCSC](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/50d1272e98b44b3f93dc4865833860f5.pdf?productCode=C114599) depicts `817` and an anode marker; omit unverifiable production codes and treat font, arrangement and any omitted logo as illustrative.

The drawing's extracted text suggests a gull-wing package with 2.54 mm lead pitch, body approximately 4.6 × 6.5 × 3.5 mm, and lead span approximately 10.16 mm. These dimensions require visual confirmation from the actual drawing: the PDF extraction mixes several views and the current manufacturer's diagram is not machine-readable. Do not select an ordinary through-hole DIP or socket merely because its name says DIP-4.

The repository's already-reviewed procedural `kicad_agents/display_model_onsemi_fod817_smd4.py` (SHA-256 `b3f51112c09bfa8c30b3091629fb095a1ad6fc93e1dde9b060c83b8558945fee`) uses a 6.5 × 4.6 × 3.5 mm body, 10.16 mm lead span and 2.54 mm pitch and is a promising representative model for the extracted Lite-On values. It is based on an onsemi package, so lead profile, body height and anode marker remain approximations rather than verified Lite-On geometry. The model is display-only, not a verified mechanical model or footprint. Any candidate must disclose the inferred package dimensions, source's generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions, uncertain physical details and omitted production codes, and be visually reviewed before publication.
