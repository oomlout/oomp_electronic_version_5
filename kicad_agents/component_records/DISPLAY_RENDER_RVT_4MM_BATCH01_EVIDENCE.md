# Honor RVT 4 × 5.4 mm display candidates

The manufacturer RVT specification viewed online
(https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf,
pages 4–6) calls for value, voltage and negative polarity marking and lists
4 × 5.4 mm dimensions. Individual LCSC listings confirm:

- RVT1C100M0405 / C3357: 10 µF 16 V, https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3357.html
- RVT1H1R0M0405 / C3348: 1 µF 50 V, https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3348.html
- RVT1C220M0405 / C3356: 22 µF 16 V, https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3356.html
- RVT1V4R7M0405 / C3355: 4.7 µF 35 V, https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3355.html

The KiCad polarized model has its negative sector on pad 2. Numeric text,
metal finish and sector shape are illustrative; exact ink, logo and date are
not verified. No PDF was downloaded.
