# Chengx KM 2200 µF / 16 V, 10 × 20 mm display candidate

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [LCSC C3328](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3328.html)
identifies KM228M016G20RR0VH2FP0 as a polarized 2200 µF / 16 V part with a
10 × 20 mm can and 5 mm pin spacing. The supplier's -40 to +105 °C range
differs from the local source record's -55 to +125 °C; this display work
leaves the electrical record unchanged. The [Chengx KM series document](https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/11296046.html)
also describes 105 °C operation. Black sleeve, white ink, negative stripe,
`2200uF / 16V / KM 105C` text, finish and actual artwork are illustrative.

The candidate KiCad 10 mm P5 model's straight sleeve must be extended from
its 10 mm can to 20 mm while preserving ends and leads. Confirm the real-mesh
deformation, polarity against the footprint, and visual preview before a
final render and publication. No PDF was downloaded.
