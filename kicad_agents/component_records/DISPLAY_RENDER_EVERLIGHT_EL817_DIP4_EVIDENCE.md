# Everlight EL817(B)-F DIP4 display evidence

An Everlight [certification record](https://www.everlighteurope.com/files/cto_layout/img/Everlight/Certificates/Quality%20and%20Safety/CE_2020_40284.pdf) names EL817(B)-F; its related [EL817-G series datasheet](https://en.everlight.com/wp-content/plugins/ItemRelationship/product_files/pdf/EL817-G.pdf) shows a standard four-lead DIP package. This document is for the related -G series and does not establish every mechanical or marking detail of the -F orderable. `EL817` is an illustrative family identifier, with rank, logo, date and lot codes omitted; typography, placement and orientation are illustrative.

Everlight's [catalogue](https://www.everlight.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/EVERLIGHT_2025-2026_Catalogue-.pdf) lists approximately 6.5 × 4.58 × 3.5 mm for the related EL817-G body. Catalogue source lists 10 × 5 mm, with no reference, height or footprint. The generic KiCad DIP4 model has total bounds 7.874 × 4.93 × 6.98 mm including projecting pins, and a generic end notch; real optocoupler pin-one marking and exact -F shape may differ. It is a display approximation, not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
