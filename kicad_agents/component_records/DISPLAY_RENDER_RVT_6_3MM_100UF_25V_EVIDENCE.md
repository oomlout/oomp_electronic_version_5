# Honor RVT 6.3 mm 100 µF 25 V display pilot

Reviewed 2026-11-18. [LCSC C3338](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3338.html)
identifies Honor RVT1E101M0607 as 100 µF, 25 V, 6.3 mm diameter, 7.7 mm
seated height. The [manufacturer RVT specification](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf)
pages 4–6 calls for value, voltage and negative polarity and lists the
6.3 × 7.7 mm case. Exact ink, layout, maker logo and date are not verified;
`100` / `25V` is an illustrative numeric marking.

KiCad CP_Elec_6.3x7.7 STEP has a dark top sector on positive X, aligned to
pad 2 of its footprint, the negative pin of the KiCad polarized symbol.
The metal finish, sector and body proportions are illustrative. No PDF was
downloaded.
