# Wuxi I-CORE AiP74LVC1T45GC363.TR SOT363 display evidence

A Wuxi I-CORE-authored [datasheet mirrored by HQEW](https://dfsimg3.hqewimg.com/group5/M00/02/F9/wKhk3mLxxqqASB3JABb1MxqhxWc477.pdf) lists exact AiP74LVC1T45GC363.TR as SOT363 with 2.1 × 1.3 mm body, 0.65 mm pitch and `CXXX` marking. X denotes variable content; rendering just `C` is an illustrative incomplete marking with variable characters omitted. Typeface, orientation and placement are illustrative.

Catalogue MPN is present but manufacturer field is absent; Wuxi I-CORE identity is inferred from catalogue ID and manufacturer-authored ordering table. Catalogue lists 2.0 × 1.25 mm and omits height and footprint. Generic KiCad SOT-363 SC-70-6 has 2.1 × 2.0 × 1.1 mm overall bounds and is a display approximation, not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
