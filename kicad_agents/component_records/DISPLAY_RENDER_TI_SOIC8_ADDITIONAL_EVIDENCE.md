Both current catalogue records identify Texas Instruments and the KiCad
`Package_SO:SOIC-8_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm` machine footprint, with body length
4.9 mm and width 3.9 mm. Neither source record specifies height. The TI D0008A
outline gives a 1.75 mm maximum seated height; this is a maximum, not an
individual source value. Recheck the exact source identity and nominal KiCad
model hash, bounds and pin-one orientation before staging a recipe. TI lists
the top-marking strings, but layout, font, logos and production date/lot details
remain unverified and must be disclosed as illustrative. No PDF was downloaded.
