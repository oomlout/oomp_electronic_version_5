# Chengx KM 10 × 17 mm radial candidates

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [LCSC C3336](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3336.html)
identifies KM107M100G17RR0VH2FP0 as 100 µF / 100 V, and
[LCSC C5300](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C5300.html) identifies
KM156M400G17RR0VH2FP0 as 15 µF / 400 V. Both are 10 × 17 mm radial cans
with 5 mm lead spacing. C5300 is explicitly polarized in the listing; verify
C3336 polarity against its electrical record and footprint before rendering.
The local records' -55 to +125 °C operating range conflicts with the KM series
and C5300 listing's -40 to +105 °C. Display work does not change source data.

The sleeve colours, text arrangement, typography, stripe and finish remain
illustrative; the exact production artwork has not been established. The
10 × 17 mm model adaptation and marking must be inspected for each part, with
negative stripe matched to pad 2. The decimal-free `15uF / 400V / KM 105C`
and `100uF / 100V / KM 105C` layouts are examples, not manufacturer artwork.
No PDF was downloaded. Do not publish until the pilot geometry passes and each
individual preview and final image has been checked.
