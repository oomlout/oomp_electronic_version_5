# Xucheng descriptive 200 mA glass fuse display research

The catalogue records the distributor's descriptive manufacturer part number
`0.2A 250VACGlass Tube Fuse`, not a conventional manufacturer identifier.
The source associates it with 200 mA, 250 V and a nominal 5.2 × 20 mm glass
tube. Its existing distributor-provided PDF (SHA-256
`ae0df5fc80a70b214fcb8288a0c1a699cb8f496dce3dcb881793186a2b365880`)
was visually reviewed. It shows generic 20 mm glass fuses and nickel-plated
brass caps, but does not identify the exact manufacturer or MPN and its rating
table starts at 250 mA; it does not verify a 200 mA variant. Therefore the
identity and 200 mA/250 V rating are taken from the source's distributor row,
not claimed as verified by this PDF.

The existing 5.2 × 20 mm glass display model can illustrate the general tube,
with a visible internal element. `200mA 250V` is an illustrative rating-based
end-cap inscription, not a verified production marking. Exact package geometry,
cap and wire dimensions, finish, and marking placement remain approximate.
The source 6.5 mm height conflicts with its 5.2 mm cylindrical diameter. The
display is not manufacturer CAD or a PCB footprint.
