# Xucheng 5G.3000210000S1 glass fuse display research

The source record identifies Xucheng 5G.3000210000S1, 3 A, 250 V, nominal
5.2 × 20 mm. No PDF is attached locally. An online
[Xucheng-authored specification](https://www.unikeyic.com/media/datasheet/ca/14/649f/ca/d7a8497c2b22ba7233151c9874cf085e.pdf)
was read without downloading it. Its cover identifies the exact MPN, 5 × 20
mm, 3 A / 250 V and product inscription `5G3A 250V`. Pages 4–5 describe a
quick-blow glass tube with visible alloy wire and nickel-plated caps; page 5
shows the same inscription on the cap. A [distributor listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3118.html)
independently identifies this part as 5.2 × 20 mm.

The display uses the reviewed 5.2 × 20 mm glass model and documented
`5G3A 250V` text. Cap shape, wire and glass dimensions, typeface and placement
are illustrative; manufacturer/approval marks are omitted. The catalogue's
6.5 mm height conflicts with a 5.2 mm cylindrical diameter. This is display
geometry, not manufacturer CAD or a PCB footprint. The linked specification
was read online; no PDF was downloaded.
