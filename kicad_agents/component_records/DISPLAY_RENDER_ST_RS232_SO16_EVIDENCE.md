# ST SO16 RS-232 display evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. ST order tables identify each exact SO16 orderable and listed marking. ST202ECDR and ST202CDR both list ST202C; ST232EBDR lists ST202B. ST SO16 drawing specifies 9.8–10.0 mm length, 3.8–4.0 mm body width, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.75 mm maximum height. Catalogue lists 9.9 × 3.9 mm but omits height and footprint. KiCad SOIC-16 model is illustrative, not a verified footprint. Typeface, placement and omitted logo/lot/date marks are illustrative.
