The [JLCPCB listing for C37208](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/2.54-1x6P%E7%9B%B4%E9%92%88/C37208) identifies one row of six straight through-hole pins at 2.54 mm, black insulator, gold-plated brass, 2.5 mm insulation height and 3 mm tails. Its structured mating-pin length of 5.7 mm differs from the same listing's 6 mm summary. This distributor listing is secondary evidence and does not establish the exact finish or geometry of every batch.

Gold and body material are illustrative; the model is not manufacturer CAD or a verified mechanical footprint.
