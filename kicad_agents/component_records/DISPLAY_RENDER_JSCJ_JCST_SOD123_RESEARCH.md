# JSCJ catalogue records with JCST example datasheets

The catalogue attributes these SOD-123 diodes to Jiangsu Changjing Electronics Technology Co., Ltd. (JSCJ). The locally stored documents instead identify Jiangsu Changjiang Electronics Technology Co., Ltd. (JCST); do not claim the local documents confirm JSCJ factory markings.

* The MMSZ5235B local PDF, SHA-256 `f0ca04b4c091d9d68a770423b0d44c5d87e4cd92028903d95211a7aa446d6323`, has two pages. Its page 1 illustrates SOD-123 and a cathode band; page 2 lists JCST examples MMSZ5235B `E5`, MMSZ5238B `F3`, MMSZ5245B `H5` and MMSZ5248B `J3`. It does not include an explicit mechanical drawing. The [LCSC MMSZ5235B listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C2124.html) and [MMSZ5245B listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/Zener-Diodes_Changjiang-Electronics-Tech-CJ-MMSZ5245B_C2126.html) describe catalogue JSCJ parts in SOD-123; the listings do not establish those factory codes.
* The B0530W local PDF, SHA-256 `cbe9ff977af6cd8a640d6113893247e60a7c55a69beec9caf2664908e3209af0`, page 1, identifies JCST's B0530W code as `SE` and illustrates a striped SOD-123; its two pages contain no mechanical drawing.
* The 1N4148W local PDF, SHA-256 `358627a9f88dd139c2f0c88662aa8af98992a2bfb47266b79e05e07b29385cfa`, page 1, identifies JCST BAV16W/1N4148W and lists `T6,T4` together without explicitly mapping the codes to the two devices. It illustrates a striped SOD-123, and its two pages contain no mechanical drawing. JSCJ's [AD-BAV16W/1N4148W datasheet](https://www.jscj-elec.com/gallery/file/AD-BAV16W_1N4148W.pdf), page 1, separately maps `T4` with an overbar over the T to **AD-1N4148W**. That is a related automotive variant, not confirmation for the catalogue's unsuffixed part. Any plain `T4` illustration must disclose the omitted overbar and variant limitation.

If represented using the KiCad SOD-123 model and shared JSCJ BZT52 scene, these codes must be explicitly identified as illustrative different-manufacturer examples, and the absence of a local dimensioned drawing must be disclosed. The independent BZT52 pilot and shared-pass comparison remain mandatory before scene reuse. Do not change the source manufacturer or silently assert the JCST codes as verified JSCJ codes.
