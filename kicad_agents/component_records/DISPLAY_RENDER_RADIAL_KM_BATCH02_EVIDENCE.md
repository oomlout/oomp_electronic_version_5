# CX KM 8 × 12 mm radial display candidates

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [LCSC C2772](https://www.lcsc.com/product-image/C2772.html)
and the [manufacturer KM document](https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/11296046.html)
identify KM107M063F12RR0VH2FP0, a 100 µF / 63 V 8 × 12 mm part;
[LCSC C2051](https://item.szlcsc.com/2403.html) identifies KM337M025F12RR0VH2FP0,
330 µF / 25 V, 8 × 12 mm with 3.5 mm lead spacing. The 63 V part's
manufacturer ordering information indicates the same case and series; its
3.5 mm spacing is also recorded in the local source. Both are polarized.

The `100uF / 63V / KM 105C` and `330uF / 25V / KM 105C` layouts are
illustrative, as are black sleeve, white ink, stripe shape and finish.
Exact production artwork is unverified. The KM series document gives 105 °C
operation, whereas the source records list a different temperature range;
the electrical records are not changed by this display. KiCad's 8 mm model
has only its straight sleeve stretched to 12 mm; leads and end details remain
unscaled. Pad 2 is negative. No PDF was downloaded.
