# Honor RVT 8 × 10.2 mm display candidates

The RVT manufacturer specification for another value, viewed online at
https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf
pages 4–6, calls for the nominal value, rated voltage and negative polarity
mark, and lists the 8 × 10.2 mm case family. These listings identify the
individual selected components:

- RVT1V221M0810 / C3340, 220 µF 35 V:
  https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/Aluminum-Electrolytic-Capacitors-SMD_Honor-Elec_C3340.html
- RVT1C471M0810 / C3341, 470 µF 16 V:
  https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3341.html

The KiCad polarized 8 × 10 mm model is scaled to 10.2 mm including its base.
Its dark sector lies on pad 2, the negative pin. The numeric two-line text,
metal finish and stripe shape are illustrative; exact text arrangement, logo
and production code are unverified. No manufacturer PDF was downloaded.
