# Huaxing KNP2W 10 Ω display evidence

The [selected Huaxing KNP2W-10Ω±5%T / C714512](https://www.lcsc.com/de/product-detail/C714512.html) is a 10 Ω, 5%, 2 W wirewound part with a 15.5 × 5 mm body. The generic KiCad DIN0516 body matches those nominal dimensions, with leads formed on 20.32 mm centres. The existing quarter-watt catalogue ID, 6.5 × 2.5 mm dimensions and 7.62 mm footprint describe a much smaller part and need separate catalogue correction.

Brown, black, black, gold is a standard four-band 10 Ω, 5% representation. The selected Huaxing part's exact band count, order and spacing have not been confirmed: other wirewound families sometimes include an additional type band. The beige coating is illustrative. The supplier listing was reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed.
