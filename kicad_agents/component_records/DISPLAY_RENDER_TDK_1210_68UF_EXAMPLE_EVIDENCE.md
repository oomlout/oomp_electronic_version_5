# Generic 1210 68 µF capacitor: TDK display example

Reviewed 2026-11-19. The generic catalogue item has no selected manufacturer,
part number or electrical ratings. Its name and KiCad footprint say 1210
(3.2 × 2.5 mm), but its `dimensions_mm` instead says 10 × 5 mm. This is an
unresolved source-record conflict and is not silently corrected by a display.

[TDK C3225X5R0J686M200AC](https://product.tdk.com/en/search/capacitor/ceramic/mlcc/info?part_no=C3225X5R0J686M200AC)
is an example 68 µF, 6.3 V, X5R 1210 ceramic; [TDK's published C-series
table](https://media.digikey.com/pdf/Data%20Sheets/TDK%20PDFs/C_Series_Rev2018.pdf)
lists its 3225 size and nominal 2.00 ± 0.20 mm thickness. The example's
6.3 V and dielectric must not be asserted as ratings of the generic item.
Its production status and a physical appearance drawing have not been verified.
Show an illustrative unmarked ceramic model, disclose the TDK example and
the catalogue dimension conflict prominently on the display page, and do not
change source dimensions, ratings, or procurement fields. The colour and
terminal finish are illustrative. No PDF was downloaded.
