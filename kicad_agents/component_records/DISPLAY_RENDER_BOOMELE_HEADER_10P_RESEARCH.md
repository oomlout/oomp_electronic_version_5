# BOOMELE ten-position 2.54 mm header display research

The source record `electronic_connector_header_2_54_mm_pitch_through_hole_10_pin` identifies BOOMELE `2.54-1*10P针`, records 25.4 × 2.48 mm and selects `Connector_PinHeader_2.54mm:PinHeader_1x10_P2.54mm_Vertical`. The [JLCPCB C57369 listing](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/2.54-1%2A10P/C57369) identifies a black 1×10 through-hole header at 2.54 mm pitch with brass contacts. It is secondary distributor evidence; it does not establish exact BOOMELE pin length or plating.

The locally stored PDF SHA-256 `e942aeb6ead33cfe1b159125f66b75ddf7c706f92a218971a789ec760a109b11` is by Shenzhen Cankemeng, not BOOMELE. Its second page shows the B-2100SXXP-A110 straight header family with 2.5 mm insulator, 6 mm mating pins, 3 mm tails and gold-over-nickel contacts, and its table includes a ten-position variant. It is a different-manufacturer dimensional example only and cannot establish BOOMELE's exact dimensions or finish. No PDF was downloaded for this review.

The generic KiCad `Connector_PinHeader_2.54mm.3dshapes/PinHeader_1x10_P2.54mm_Vertical.step` has SHA-256 `a2f7a9c513c3be0cbd5de8cdd22fdbf1c2e3510c72d682fd089d252fe71b9dc7`, measures 2.54 × 25.4 × 11.54 mm and has 64 dark-body and 180 gold-coloured terminal faces. Its header credits 2017 kicad StepUp under CC BY-SA 4.0 with the KiCad library exception. Its generic 6 mm mating pins and 3 mm tails match the Cankemeng example, not an exact BOOMELE drawing, while the 2.54 mm body width differs from the source's 2.48 mm. Any gold finish is illustrative, and the different-manufacturer PDF and unverified exact BOOMELE pin lengths must be disclosed; the model is not manufacturer CAD or a verified footprint.
