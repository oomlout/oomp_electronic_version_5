# Microchip ATtiny404-SSNR SOIC-14 display evidence

Microchip [product information](https://onlinedocs.microchip.com/oxy/GUID-5A56DB3A-31E1-4F46-984F-39186535C84E-en-US-7/GUID-CC06B137-262F-43C8-90FD-A14C59BF9C29.html) lists ATtiny404-SSNR in 14-lead SOIC. The catalogue ID encodes this orderable, but manufacturer and MPN fields are missing; the represented Microchip identity is inferred from the ID. The catalogue specifies 8.69 × 3.9 × 1.6 mm and a SOIC-14 footprint. The generic KiCad SOIC-14 model is scaled from 1.75 to 1.6 mm overall height to approximate the catalogue and is not a verified mechanical model.

Microchip's [automotive errata](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MCU08/ProductDocuments/Errata/ATtiny202-204-402-404-406-Auto-SilConErrataClarif-DS80000864.pdf) illustrates `TINY404` on a 14-pin SOIC automotive device. It does not establish the marking for this exact industrial orderable, so this is an illustrative family identifier; logo, variable date and lot codes are omitted, and typography, orientation and placement are illustrative.
