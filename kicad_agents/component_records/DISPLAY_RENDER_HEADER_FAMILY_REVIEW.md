# 2.54 mm straight-header rendering review

Inventory on 2026-11-21 found a range of `electronic_connector_header_2_54_mm_pitch_through_hole_<N>_pin` records with straight, single-row KiCad footprints. The manufacturer and part number vary between records. A generic model is not automatically an exact representation of each distributor part: review count, rows, orientation, pin lengths, colour and finish for every candidate before staging it. The existing generated header reference assets are nominal and are not manufacturer CAD.

Two specific source conflicts need resolution before an exact-part render:

* The eight-pin entry names XFCN `PZ254V-12-8P`, while its selected footprint is `PinHeader_1x08_P2.54mm_Vertical`. [LCSC's listing for C492421](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C492421.html) identifies the named component as **2 x 4**, not 1 x 8. The [XFCN PZ254V-12 family drawing](https://www.xfconn.com/uploads/soft/20240711/4-240G115431Ea.pdf) describes a double-row series. Do not render the 1 x 8 model as the named manufacturer part without resolving the source association.
* The 18-pin entry names XFCN `PZ254R-11-18P`, while its footprint is `PinHeader_1x18_P2.54mm_Vertical`. The [manufacturer's PZ254R-11 family drawing](https://doc.chipmall.com/datasheet/rev_2503061537_xfcn-pz254r-11-18p_c63536925.pdf) depicts bent, right-angle pins. Do not render the straight footprint as the named part without resolving the source association.
* Entries 23–27 and 33–35 name XKB `X6511WR` series components while their selected footprints are vertical. [A family distributor listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C706869.html) identifies an X6511WR variant as right-angle, and a [separate design using the exact 24-pin part](https://www.holtek.com/webapi/5905185/WAS-24B1EN_ReferenceDesign.pdf) identifies a horizontal footprint. Treat each named variant as a suspected orientation conflict pending its own drawing; the two cited examples do not independently prove every suffix.
* The 30-pin entry names Amphenol `54101-T30-00LF`. The [manufacturer's product page](https://www.amphenol-cs.com/product/54101t3000lf.html) specifies 8.08 mm mating length, 3.05 mm tail and tin plating, rather than the generic 6 mm gold-coloured example. The page itself inconsistently reports zero positions and one contact, while [the LCSC listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C5156075.html) lists 30. Check the actual manufacturer drawing and CAD before representing that part.

These are evidence findings, not a decision that either generic catalogue entry should be deleted or renamed. Other distributors or intended uses may explain the associations. Do not silently change manufacturer data, the footprint or connector taxonomy as part of a display-render batch. No manufacturer PDF was downloaded.

The 10-position BOOMELE record has a local PDF, but its first two pages identify Shenzhen Cankemeng and the B-2100SXXP-A110 family, a different manufacturer. The drawing is a useful straight, single-row 2.54 mm example with 6 mm mating pins, 3 mm tails and gold-over-nickel contacts; it does not verify the exact BOOMELE part or its finish. Do not cite this file as BOOMELE manufacturer evidence.
