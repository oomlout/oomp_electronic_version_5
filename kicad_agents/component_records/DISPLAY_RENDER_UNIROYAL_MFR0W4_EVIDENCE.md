# UNI-ROYAL MFR0W4 quarter-watt display evidence

The reviewed selected parts are [MFR0W4F1202A50, 12 kΩ, 1%](https://item.szlcsc.com/59715.html)
and [MFR0W4F8202A50, 82 kΩ, 1%](https://www.lcsc.com/fr/product-detail/C58623.html).
The manufacturer-authored [UniOhm precision metal-film sheet](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/lcsc/Uniroyal-Elec-MFR02SF2401A10_C63173.pdf)
identifies MFR0W4 as 1/4 W, 6.5 ±1.0 mm long and 2.2 ±0.3 mm diameter,
and specifies EIA colour coding. The [UNI-ROYAL precision metal-film marking
sheet](https://www.uni-royal.cn/en/images/userfile/file/17539567746e541b3e9bf727c1.pdf)
page 3 documents five-band 1% coding; the fifth band is brown. Therefore:

- 12 kΩ: brown, red, black, red, brown.
- 82 kΩ: grey, red, black, red, brown.

KiCad's DIN0207 body is approximately 6.3 × 2.5 mm, with the diameter at the
manufacturer's upper limit. Bent leads and the traditional beige coating are
illustrative and do not assert a production-lot colour or lead forming. The
specifications were reviewed online; no local PDFs are claimed.
