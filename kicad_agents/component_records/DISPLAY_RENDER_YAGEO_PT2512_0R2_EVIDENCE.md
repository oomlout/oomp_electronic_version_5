# Yageo PT2512FK-7W0R2L 0.2 Ω display evidence

The previous FH RPL-12L2R00FT / C5119990 selection was 2 Ω, not this
catalogue ID's 0.2 Ω. It was replaced by Yageo PT2512FK-7W0R2L / C309535,
whose [manufacturer specification](https://www.yageogroup.com/component-documentation/download/specsheet/PT2512FK-7W0R2L)
and [LCSC listing](https://www.lcsc.com/de/product-detail/C309535.html) give
0.2 Ω, 1%, 2 W and 2512. This is a current-sense part; matching nominal
resistance, package, tolerance and power does not prove other characteristics
are interchangeable with an intended design. The former identity is retained
in source research notes and its original record is saved locally.

[Yageo's PT-series specification](https://yageogroup.com/content/datasheet/asset/file/PYU-PT-GROUP_521_ROHS_L)
page 3 gives four-character markings for PT2512, with R as decimal point;
`R200` denotes 0.2 Ω. Page 4 gives 6.35 ±0.10 × 3.20 ±0.15 × 0.55 ±0.10 mm.
The KiCad STEP model measures 6.30 × 3.20 × 0.60 mm, within those tolerances.
Generic model geometry, materials, typeface and ink placement are illustrative.
Online manufacturer PDFs were reviewed without making local PDF claims.
