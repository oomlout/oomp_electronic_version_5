# RALEC RTT01 and RTT02 display evidence

The manufacturer-authored [RTT thick-film specification IE-SP-010](https://www.bdtic.com/DataSheet/RALEC/IE-SP-010.pdf),
released 20 February 2011 and reviewed online, states in section 9.4 that
RTT01 (0201) and RTT02 (0402) bodies are unmarked. Its dimensions table and
resistance-range table cover the nominal KiCad models and the selected values.
Check exact primary manufacturer, MPN, value, tolerance, footprint and supplier
record for each part. The display geometry, materials and colour are generic;
the reused image represents one supplier choice.

The online specification was not downloaded. An existing local RALEC family
sheet is available from `parts_source/electronic_resistor_0402_82_ohm/datasheet.pdf`;
it is an older IE-SP-007 edition and should be identified separately rather
than described as a copy of the online IE-SP-010 specification.
