# Shanghai Belling BL1117 and ST LD1117 SOT-223 research

The catalogue contains `BL1117-33CX` with manufacturer `BL(Shanghai Belling)`, SOT-223-3 TabPin2 footprint, and generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions. A [Shanghai Belling BL1117 data sheet supplied by LCSC](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/lcsc/2304140030_BL-Shanghai-Belling-BL1117-33CX_C5400.pdf) identifies output code 33 as 3.3 V and package code X as SOT-223. Its marking diagram includes the product code and voltage code along with variable production data; confirm diagram placement before choosing a render marking. Another [historical manufacturer-authored data sheet](https://file2.dzsc.com/product/19/10/24/59960_153427263.pdf) explicitly lists BL1117-33CX as a 3.3 V SOT-223 orderable. Do not assume an exact lot or date marking.

The `st_ld1117_3_3` catalogue entry is a generic 3.3 V LD1117 family entry: it lacks an exact manufacturer/orderable field and footprint, though ST and the family are implied by its ID. [ST's LD1117 data sheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/ld1117.pdf), package drawing and order-code table identify SOT-223 3.3 V members including LD1117S33TR and LD1117S33CTR. Do not claim either exact orderable is the one in the catalogue. The catalogue's package drawing depicts two small terminals on each side, conflicting with the manufacturer SOT-223 drawing's three small leads and one tab. Any display must explicitly disclose this conflict and its generic family representation; verify physical top marking separately.

The already-reviewed KiCad `Package_TO_SOT_SMD.3dshapes/SOT-223.step` model has 7 × 6.5 × 1.7 mm overall bounds and SHA-256 `77ad6dbacc493cb619c728f26d2ddcf461eb0feb6b977b47d01d51e67016f654`. BL's 10 × 5 mm dimensions have no documented reference and must be disclosed as inconsistent. For the BL candidate, use `BL1117` and `33` only as an illustrative identification derived from the data sheet; precise top-line placement, logo, font and lot/date/temperature details remain unverified. Preserve existing records and do not claim this model is a verified footprint. The generic ST candidate is not staged.
