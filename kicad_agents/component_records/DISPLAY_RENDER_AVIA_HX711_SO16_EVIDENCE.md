# AVIA HX711 SOP-16L display evidence

AVIA Semiconductor's [datasheet mirrored by ProgLogic](https://www.proglogic.eu/web/TechDoc/792/hx711_english.pdf) identifies HX711 in SOP-16L and gives nominal 9.9 mm length, 3.9 mm body width, 6.0 mm lead span, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.2–1.6 mm package thickness. The catalogue ID names AVIA/HX711 but manufacturer and MPN fields are missing, so represented identity is inferred. The exact top marking was not established; `HX711` is an illustrative family identifier, with logo and variable production details omitted. Typeface, orientation and placement are illustrative.

Catalogue lists 9.9 × 3.9 mm without height or footprint. The generic KiCad SOIC-16 model is scaled from 1.75 to 1.5 mm as a display approximation; it is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
