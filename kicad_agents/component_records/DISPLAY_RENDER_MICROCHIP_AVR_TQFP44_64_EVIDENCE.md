# Microchip AVR TQFP-44 and TQFP-64 display research

Microchip's [ATmega16A summary](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/Atmel-8154-8-bit-AVR-ATmega16A_Datasheet%20Summary.pdf) identifies `ATmega16A-AU` as package 44A. Its manufacturer-authored [package drawing](https://d25vv4z8gtre3w.cloudfront.net/fajlcsatolas/2985.pdf) gives a 10 × 10 mm body, nominal 12 × 12 mm overall lead span, 0.8 mm pitch and 1.20 mm maximum overall height. The [ATmega32A data sheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/Atmega32A-DataSheet-Complete-DS40002072A.pdf) identifies `ATmega32A-AU` in the same 44A TQFP package.

Microchip's [ATmega128A data sheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/DeviceDoc/Atmel-8151-8-bit-AVR-ATmega128A_Datasheet.pdf) identifies `ATmega128A-AU` in package 64A, with 14 × 14 mm body, nominal 16 × 16 mm overall lead span, 0.8 mm pitch and 1.20 mm maximum overall height.

All three catalogue records specify exact manufacturer and orderable, but list generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions without a reference and no selected footprint. Disclose the discrepancy rather than treating the stored dimensions as verified. The inspected KiCad `TQFP-44_10x10mm_P0.8mm.step` has SHA-256 `61ecec1147f835d2c9d165c450b1c8d53e65b038f6ca1dac3066a2dfb1bbbb85`, 12 × 12 × 1.1 mm lead-inclusive bounds and distinct body, lead and pin-indicator colours. Inspect the separate `TQFP-64_14x14mm_P0.8mm.step` hash, bounds and colours before use. Top text may only be a family illustration unless the exact physical marking is established; omit unverified logos, dates and lot codes. Display models are not verified mechanical models or footprints.
