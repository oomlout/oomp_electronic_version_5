# MaxLinear RS-232 SO-16 display evidence

Manufacturer datasheets confirm each exact orderable and NSOIC-16 package. Their narrow SOIC drawings specify 3.90 mm nominal body width, 6.00 mm nominal overall width, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.75 mm maximum overall height. The catalogue lists 9.9 × 3.9 mm without height or footprint; the KiCad SOIC-16 model is illustrative and not a verified PCB footprint.
