# Microchip serial EEPROM SOIC-8 display evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. The manufacturer datasheets show the example first
marking lines below on industrial, narrow SOIC packages, with package code,
date and traceability on later lines. The display shows only the first line;
the other lines and logo are omitted, and font and placement are illustrative.
Tape-and-reel is an ordering suffix. Both local records omit height.
Microchip's individual 8-lead SN outline drawings use 1.27 mm pitch,
approximately 4.9 × 3.9 mm body and 1.75 mm maximum height, matching the
reviewed KiCad model nominally 6.0 × 4.9 × 1.75 mm overall. No PDF was downloaded.
