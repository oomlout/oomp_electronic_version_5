their individual TI datasheets. Each datasheet contains the D0014A 14-pin SOIC
outline: 8.55–8.75 mm body length, 3.8–4.0 mm body width, 5.8–6.2 mm typical

8.7 × 3.9 × 1.75 mm and the matching KiCad footprint. The reviewed KiCad model

placement, orientation and manufacturing codes remain illustrative.
