# YXC HC-49US through-hole crystal: initial rendering evidence

The 10 MHz source record identifies YXC X49SD10MSD2SC. Its local `datasheet.pdf` (SHA-256 `8d9dbc91b68d398acdebaf93a00d8b21ceca8cb94e6b12e62b564e2194ada5c0`) was visually reviewed. It is a YXC HC-49US DIP family drawing, not an exact orderable part drawing. It depicts an oval metal can with two through-hole leads, 11.5 mm maximum length, 4.5 ± 0.2 mm width, 3.6 ± 0.3 mm can height, 4.88 ± 0.25 mm lead spacing and 6.0 ± 0.5 mm lead length. It gives a standard frequency range of 3.2768–32 MHz. A generic “Marking” label appears on the can; it does not establish an exact marking, typography or placement for this 10 MHz record. Any frequency text used for illustration must be disclosed as illustrative.

The record refers to KiCad `Crystal:Crystal_HC49-U_Vertical`; the model must be dimensionally and visually checked against the HC-49US low-profile drawing, since the similar HC-49U naming is not proof of a matching can height. Do not stage or publish a render until geometry, material roles, top marking projection and source identity have been checked. Other frequency variants may use the family drawing as family evidence, but each requires source and marking validation.
