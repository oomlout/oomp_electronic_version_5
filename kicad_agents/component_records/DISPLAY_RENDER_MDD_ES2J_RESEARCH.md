# MDD ES2J SMA display research

The existing local MDD ES2A–ES2J manufacturer datasheet, SHA-256
`b3a7e1d0f678d0f19e646287a373916161994dda07e31e658ab7cbac28d7f778`,
page 1 was rendered and visually inspected. It identifies the ES2J as
DO-214AC/SMA, shows 3.80–4.50 mm body length, 2.30–2.80 mm body width,
1.85–2.45 mm height, 4.70–5.30 mm overall terminal span and a cathode
band. The table's marking-code row appears blank; ES2J text in the display
is an illustrative identification, not a verified physical marking.

The KiCad `D_SMA.step` SHA-256
`1cfddd4e3b1bf22b8fc6ee3258c2aade882ed7b85b528e23b9db42437775a1db`
has 5.0 × 2.7 × 2.22 mm overall bounds; its body extends approximately
0.06 mm beyond the drawing maximum. The source record's 10 × 5 mm dimensions
and missing footprint are inconsistent with the manufacturer drawing.
Preserve the record; this display geometry and lettering are illustrative,
not manufacturer CAD or a PCB footprint. No PDF was downloaded.
