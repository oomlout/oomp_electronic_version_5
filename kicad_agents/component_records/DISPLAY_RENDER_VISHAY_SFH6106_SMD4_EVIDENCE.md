# Vishay SFH6106-1X001T SMD-4 display evidence

[Vishay's SFH6106 datasheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/83666/sfh610a.pdf) identifies exact SFH6106-1X001T as SMD-4, with 6.48–6.81 × 4.55–4.83 mm body, 3.30–3.81 mm body height, 9.53–10.03 mm lead span, and 2.41–2.67 mm lead pitch. The model uses midpoints and approximates the lead profiles and pin-one marker. SFH6106 is an illustrative family identifier; exact package marking, manufacturer logo, CTR rank, date and lot codes are omitted or unverified.

The catalogue source gives 10 × 5 mm without dimension reference, height or footprint. The procedural mesh is a display approximation and is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
