# VO metal oxide 1.2 MΩ display evidence

The selected [VO MO1/4W-1M2±5%-ST52](https://jlc.com.vn/linh-kien/through-hole-resistors/mo1-4w-1m25-st52-vo/) is 1.2 MΩ, 5%, 250 mW, with a listed 6.2 × 2.7 mm body. The generic KiCad model has a 6.3 × 2.5 mm body and formed leads, and is an approximation.

The [VO MO-series specification text](https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/12269180.html) describes MIL/EIA colour coding and gray coating for normal size parts. A standard four-band representation is brown, red, green, gold. The exact selected item's band count and placement have not been verified; the traditional beige coating is illustrative. The specification was reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed.
