# Yageo AC2512FK-0713R3L 13.3 Ω display evidence

The former Stackpole RMEF2512FT133R / C6981998 selection was 133 Ω, not
this catalogue ID's 13.3 Ω. It was replaced with the [Yageo AC2512FK-0713R3L
/ JLCPCB C3925735](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/YAGEO-AC2512FK0713R3L/C3925735),
which is 13.3 Ω, 1%, 1 W and 2512. Other characteristics require application
review; this is not a claim of full electrical interchangeability. The former
selection remains in source research notes and its original record is backed up.

[Yageo's AC-series specification](https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/447/PYu_AC_51_RoHS_L_11-3418659.pdf)
pages 2–4 describes four-character 1% AC2512 markings, the decimal notation,
and nominal body dimensions 6.35 ±0.10 × 3.10 ±0.15 × 0.55 ±0.10 mm.
`13R3` represents 13.3 Ω. The generic KiCad model measures 6.30 × 3.20 ×
0.60 mm, inside those stated tolerances; its geometry, materials, ink and
placement remain illustrative. The manufacturer specification was reviewed
online; no local PDF is claimed.
