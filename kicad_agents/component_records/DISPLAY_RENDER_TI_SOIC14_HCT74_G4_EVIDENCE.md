# TI SN74HCT74DRG4 SOIC-14 evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-19. TI's earlier revision E datasheet [archived manufacturer document](https://ormix.lv/files/item_uploads/770bad0b0ca66bf7f1e3013929d8d60b.pdf), package option addendum, explicitly lists SN74HCT74DRG4, SOIC D 14, marking HCT74. TI's [current revision G](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hct74.pdf) no longer lists that exact G4 suffix, but lists SN74HCT74DR with marking HCT74 and the same SOIC D 14 package. The D0014A drawing specifies 8.55–8.75 × 3.8–4.0 mm body, 1.27 mm pitch, 1.75 mm maximum height. Local source matches footprint and 8.7 × 3.9 × 1.75 mm. The marking is based on the historical exact-orderable table; typography, placement and variable lot details are illustrative.
