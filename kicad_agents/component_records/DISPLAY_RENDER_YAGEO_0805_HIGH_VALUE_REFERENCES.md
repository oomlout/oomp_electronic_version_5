# 0805 high-value Yageo visual references

Both selected FOJAN records have manufacturer part numbers truncated to `TS`.
Their original identities and appearances are unverified. Images illustrate
the named Yageo references; matching nominal value, tolerance, package and
power do not establish full electrical interchangeability. KiCad geometry,
materials and marking typography are illustrative.

[Yageo RC_L specification](https://yageogroup.com/content/datasheet/asset/file/PYU-RC_GROUP_51_ROHS_L),
page 3, specifies three-digit E24 markings for 5% 0805 packages. Reference
identities and ratings were reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed.
