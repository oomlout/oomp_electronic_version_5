# Infineon IR2156STRPBF SOIC-14 display evidence

The [Infineon product page](https://www.infineon.com/part/IR2156S) lists the exact orderable as SOIC 14N, despite an inconsistent 16-lead phrase in its introduction; its product details and [datasheet](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/49/ir2156.pdf) agree on 14 leads. Datasheet page 20 specifies 8.56–8.74 × 3.81–3.99 mm body, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.35–1.75 mm height. Catalogue dimensions 8.69 × 3.9 mm and footprint correspond; height is absent. The generic KiCad model is an illustrative display approximation.
