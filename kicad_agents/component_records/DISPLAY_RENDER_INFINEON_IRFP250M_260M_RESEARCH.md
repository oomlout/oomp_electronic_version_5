Both drawings specify lead length 19.80–20.32 mm, body length 20.80–21.10 mm and width 15.75–16.13 mm. The original KiCad model's 20.15 mm leads and 20.95 × 15.9 mm body lie within these ranges. Finish and fine detail are approximate; the model is for display, not manufacturing. Body surface bump is omitted to avoid numerical instability in the renderer.

The marking example shows an IRFPE30, a different device. IRFP250M and IRFP260M are illustrative identifiers; exact marking, typography, logo and production codes are unverified.
