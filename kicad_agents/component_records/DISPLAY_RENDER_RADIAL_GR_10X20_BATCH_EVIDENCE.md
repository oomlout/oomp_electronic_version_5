# Chengx GR 10 × 20 mm radial display candidates

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [Eleparts](https://eleparts.co.kr/goods/view?no=8158550)
identifies GR158M016G20RR0VL4FP0 as 1500 µF / 16 V, 10 × 20 mm, 5 mm pitch;
the local record identifies it as C3311. [LCSC C3312](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C3312.html)
identifies GR477V050G20RR0VH4FP0 as 470 µF / 50 V in a 10 × 20 mm can.
The [Chengx GR series document](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/f3fea3c2dc0b410b88f0b0012f339509.pdf?productCode=C45677)
illustrates black sleeves and describes 105 °C operation for the series; it is
not an exact artwork drawing for these two parts. A separate [manufacturer
specification for GR108M010F12RR0VL4FP0](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/chengxing_1000uf10v_Lcs01_link.pdf)
specifies a **green sleeve and gold print**. Its manufacturer part-number key
defines `L` as a green background, `H` as black, and `4` as gold printing.
Thus C3311's `VL4` specifies green/gold, while C3312's `VH4` specifies
black/gold. The `1500uF / 16V / GR 105C`
and `470uF / 50V / GR 105C` layouts, stripe, typography and finish are
illustrative. The real model, 20 mm sleeve extension, pad-2 negative stripe,
each preview and each final image require inspection. No PDF was downloaded.
