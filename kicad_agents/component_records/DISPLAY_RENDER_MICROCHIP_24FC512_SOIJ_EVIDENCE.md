# Microchip 24FC512T-I/SM SOIJ-8 display research

Reviewed 2026-11-20. Microchip's [24AA512/24LC512/24FC512 datasheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MPD/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/24AA512-24LC512-24FC512-512-Kbit-I2C-Serial-EEPROM-DS20001754.pdf) identifies the SM package as an eight-lead SOIJ with 5.28 mm body. Its [older marking guide](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/en/devicedoc/21754m.pdf) illustrates `24LC512` and `I/SM` for a related SOIJ part and explains the part-number marking convention; it does not provide a specific 24FC512 example. Any representation of `24FC512` would be an inferred partial marking, with temperature, package, date and lot details omitted, and must be described as such.

The local `24FC512T-I/SM` source has generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions and no height or verified footprint. Correction: the manufacturer's [C04-056 package drawing](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/package-outline-drawings/c04-00056e.pdf) shows **gull-wing leads**, not J-shaped leads. SOIJ is Microchip's JEITA/EIAJ name for this package. The drawing gives 5.26 × 5.25 mm body, 7.94 mm overall width, 1.27 mm pitch and 1.77–2.03 mm overall height. KiCad `SOIC-8_5.3x5.3mm_P1.27mm.step` SHA-256 `121aee742692bb7bc9ef49972786ecb89d1b2d62ab089ed278651a884c800331` measures 7.8 × 5.3 × 2.16 mm overall. Its display height is scaled to the manufacturer's 2.03 mm maximum. It is an illustrative model, not a verified mechanical model or footprint. Preserve the source discrepancies and marking uncertainty in the page; do not change the source record silently.
