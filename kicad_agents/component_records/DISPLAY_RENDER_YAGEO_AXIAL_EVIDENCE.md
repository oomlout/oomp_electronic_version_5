# YAGEO MFR-25 axial resistor display evidence

Reviewed online: YAGEO MFR metal film resistor product specification, V.0,
28 September 2021, manufacturer-authored PDF hosted by a distributor:
https://robu-prod-media.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2025/02/2499275.pdf

The MFR-25 body is nominally 6.3 mm long and 2.4 mm in diameter. The marking
table specifies five colour bands for 1% tolerance: three significant digits,
one multiplier, and a brown tolerance band. Verify the primary part number,
value and tolerance for each record; do not apply this to jumper or 5% parts.

The KiCad model depicts leads bent to its selected 7.62 mm board footprint.
Band spacing, coating hue, colour shades and the bent-lead presentation are
illustrative, not an exact production-lot photograph. The document was read
online; no local manufacturer PDF has been captured.
