# Yageo visual references for selected HWA CHN 0805 resistors

not been independently verified. Images explicitly illustrate Yageo references;
they do not establish the selected HWA part's appearance or complete electrical
interchangeability. Generic KiCad geometry and materials are illustrative.

online; no local PDF is claimed.
