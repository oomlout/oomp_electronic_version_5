# Infineon IR211x PDIP-14 display evidence

Infineon lists the exact PDIP-14 orderables on product pages and in the [IR2110/IR2113 datasheet](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/49/infineon-ir2110-datasheet-en.pdf) and [IR2112 datasheet](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/24/49/infineon-ir2112-ds-en.pdf). IR2112PBF is the full-lead variant; the separately listed -1 and -2 versions omit leads. Datasheets use a generic part-number placeholder, so the rendered short family identifiers are illustrative, not verified complete markings. Logos and variable date/lot codes are omitted; typeface, orientation and placement are illustrative.

Each catalogue record says 10 × 5 mm, without width reference, height or footprint. This conflicts with the generic KiCad 14-pin DIP body, about 19 mm long, which represents the manufacturer's PDIP-14 package as a display approximation. The 6.98 mm model height includes projecting pins; it is not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
