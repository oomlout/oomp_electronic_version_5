# TI TL431ACLP TO-92 research

The [TI product page](https://www.ti.com/product/TL431/part-details/TL431ACLP) identifies the exact orderable as a three-pin TO-92 (LP) part supplied in bulk. TI's [datasheet package option addendum](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tl431.pdf) specifies device marking `TL431AC`; logo, lot and date details and their layout are not established here. The same datasheet distinguishes the bulk straight-lead option from the formed-lead tape options.

The [TI package drawing](https://e2echina.ti.com/cfs-file/__key/telligent-evolution-components-attachments/00-24-01-00-00-03-31-11/TL431.pdf) gives a 4.44–5.21 mm body diameter and 4.32–5.34 mm body height, with 12.70 mm minimum straight-lead length. The source record currently gives generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions, without a dimension reference, which is not a confirmed TO-92 body size. Preserve that source data and explicitly disclose the discrepancy if a display is produced.

Next: locate and inspect a suitable straight-lead KiCad TO-92 model; verify bounds, source colours and which side is flat. The flat face is upright in a typical model: the standard top-plane marking projection is not sufficient. Confirm or implement an orientation transform and inspect a marked preview before any publication. Do not assume the manufacturer's font, logo or variable production details.

Two installed KiCad models were inspected. `TO-92_Inline_W4.0mm_Horizontal_FlatSideUp.step` has bounds 4.83 × 8.99 × 6.33 mm and SHA-256 `fafac95a27afef2c493d967341a3fa9f165ee3abf5e259ddf23badde9cd93939`; its horizontal lead form needs inspection and may not represent the bulk straight-lead orderable. `TO-92_Inline.step` has bounds 4.83 × 3.745 × 9.8 mm, SHA-256 `f0b61c32ecbf0067157adc5a3d4966ceadf5d6dafe93c51af35f76c41c71a54a`, and the expected body and metal colours. Its total height is shorter than TI's minimum straight lead length alone; if used for display, disclose shortened leads and inspect the flat face/orientation before applying any text. Neither candidate has been rendered or selected.

Face inspection of `TO-92_Inline.step` found the flat coating face at y = -1.33 mm with outward normal (0, -1, 0), spanning x = -0.746 to 3.286 mm and z = 2.5 to 7.3 mm. An orientation rotating this normal toward +Z could place that face upward; account for the lead and body placement separately and visually verify the resulting projection. Do not modify the shared renderer while existing independent renders are running, because they bind renderer code hashes.

The full source envelope is x = -1.145..3.685, y = -1.33..2.415, z = -2.5..7.3 mm. The existing reviewed rigid transform named `to220_face_up` maps (x, y, z) to (-z, x, -y), so it also maps this TO-92 flat face upward. In that pose the full x envelope is -7.3..2.5 mm and the flat body face spans -7.3..-2.5 mm; its centre is therefore 2.5 mm left of the envelope centre. A trial `offset_x_mm: -2.5` places the marking in the body face's projected region, subject to a visual preview and an independent full render. Use TI's `TL431AC` fixed marking without inventing logo or production data; placement and typography remain illustrative. The generic model has 5.0 mm straight leads below the body, shorter than TI's 12.7 mm minimum; the source's generic 10 × 5 mm dimensions are also not a documented body measurement. Both limitations must appear in the display documentation.
