# Yageo YC124-JR-0710KL array display evidence

Yageo's YC/TC product specification V.11 was reviewed online:
https://yageogroup.com/content/Resource%20Library/Datasheet/PYU-YC_TC_GROUP_51_ROHS_L.pdf

Page 3 specifies three-digit E24 markings for 5% YC124 arrays, so 10 kΩ is
marked `103`. Page 6 gives nominal dimensions 2.00 × 1.00 × 0.45 mm; the
KiCad model's 0.35 mm height is at the tolerance limit. The selected exact
part is a 10 kΩ, 5%, four-resistor, eight-terminal array:
https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C326872.html

The sheet was reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed. Geometry, materials,
ink and placement are illustrative.
