# TAJ E-case 220 µF 16 V illustrative display

Reviewed 2026-11-19. The [KYOCERA AVX TAJ series specification](https://datasheets.kyocera-avx.com/TAJ.pdf)
page 1 gives E-case nominal 7.30 × 4.30 × 4.10 mm, the positive-anode
stripe, 227 for 220 µF and C for 16 V. The marking is illustrative and
higher voltage substitutions can occur. [DigiKey's exact listing](https://www.digikey.ro/ro/products/detail/kyocera-avx/TAJE227K016RNJ/678442)
identifies TAJE227K016RNJ as 220 µF / 16 V / 2917; catalogue supplier
identifier is C7231. The KiCad Kemet X-case model is used as a generic
7343-43 shape, scaled from its 7.6 × 4.6 × 4.3 mm overall bounds to the
TAJ nominal 7.3 × 4.3 × 4.1 mm. Its shape, stripe,
colour, finish and ink layout are illustrative; no logo or date code is
invented. This is a display image, not a dimensional or purchasing source.
The specification was viewed online; no PDF was downloaded.
