# Honor RVT 5 × 5.4 mm display candidate

The [Honor RVT specification](https://xonstorage.z8.web.core.windows.net/pdf/honor_rvt1e470m0505_xonjuly20_20_link.pdf)
pages 4–6 calls for value, voltage and negative polarity and lists 5 × 5.4 mm
cases. [JLCPCB C3337](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/HonorElec-RVT1C470M0505/C3337)
identifies RVT1C470M0505 as 47 µF 16 V. The KiCad polarized model's dark
sector lies on negative pad 2. Numeric text, finish and sector shape are
illustrative; exact ink, maker logo and date are unverified. No PDF was
downloaded.
