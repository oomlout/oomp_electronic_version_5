# onsemi MJD44H11-1G and MJD45H11-1G TO-251 display research

Both catalogue records identify onsemi. The locally stored family datasheet belongs to the MJD44H11-1G source directory and has SHA-256 `4f95f0add038033491b7bc1c6a5db1e24b409d5572cc6ec42f1b33438a5d6176`. The MJD45H11-1G source directory has no local PDF; its evidence here is the same existing family PDF, not a separately downloaded file.

The family sheet identifies MJD44H11 as NPN and MJD45H11 as PNP. Page 6's ordering table explicitly lists both `-1G` orderables as package 369D; page 8's 369D IPAK outline shows three full leads. The KiCad model's approximately 6.575 × 2.31 × 6.1 mm body and 9.56 mm lead projection fit its principal drawing ranges. Its finish and details remain approximate; it is not verified manufacturing CAD.

Page 1's marking diagram specifies a fixed `J4xH11` device code with x=4 or 5 and a `G` Pb-free mark. The display therefore uses `J4` above `4H11G` for MJD44H11-1G and `J4` above `5H11G` for MJD45H11-1G. Variable assembly location, year and week are omitted; font and placement are illustrative. No local PDF was copied or downloaded for this work.
