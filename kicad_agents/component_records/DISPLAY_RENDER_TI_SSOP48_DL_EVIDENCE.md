# Texas Instruments 48-lead DL SSOP display research

TI lists the DL SSOP-48 package at approximately 15.88 × 10.35 mm overall on the product pages for [SN74LVCH16245A](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVCH16245A), [SN74LVTH16245A](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVTH16245A) and [SN74ABT162244](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74ABT162244). The [LVCH datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvch16245a.pdf) identifies SN74LVCH16245ADLR as the DL package, the [LVTH datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvth16245a.pdf) identifies SN74LVTH16245ADLR, and the [ABT datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74abt162244.pdf) identifies SN74ABT162244DLRG4. TI's [LVTH orderable report](https://www.ti.com/quality-reliability-packaging-download/es-mx/report?opn=SN74LVTH16245ADLR) lists `LVTH16245A` as its device marking; exact typeface, placement, logo and manufacturing details are not established by that report.

The [LVCH orderable report](https://www.ti.com/quality-reliability-packaging-download/es-mx/report?opn=SN74LVCH16245ADLR) lists `LVCH16245A` as the top marking, and the ABT datasheet lists `ABT162244` for SN74ABT162244DLRG4. These manufacturer-specified text strings do not establish the exact typeface, placement, logo or manufacturing details; omit unverified details and describe the appearance as illustrative.

All three source records list 10 × 5 mm without defining the measurement; this differs substantially from the TI overall package dimensions and must remain visible in each generated description. The matching KiCad candidate is `Package_SO.3dshapes/SSOP-48_7.5x15.9mm_P0.635mm.step`; inspect its hash, colours and overall bounds before use. The model is for display and is not a validated footprint.

TI's [package solutions card](https://www.ti.com/lit/sl/sszb138a/sszb138a.pdf) gives DL SSOP-48 maximum height 2.79 mm. The generic KiCad model's 2.99 mm height exceeds that; the display mesh is scaled vertically to 2.70 mm, with the original and adjusted bounds recorded in the manifest. The scale is an approximation, not a mechanically verified model.
