# YAGEO 0603 100 nF capacitor display

The existing generic part selects YAGEO CC0603KRX7R9BB104 and LCSC C14663.
The [manufacturer specification](https://yageogroup.com/download/specsheet/CC0603KRX7R9BB104)
confirms 100 nF, X7R, ±10%, 50 V and 0603 size. The existing local datasheet is
`parts_source/electronic_capacitor_0603_100_nano_farad/datasheet.pdf`.

The KiCad 0603 model is a nominal 1.6 × 0.8 × 0.8 mm package. Its ceramic
colour and metallic finish are illustrative. The body is shown without a
marking; the exact selected part's no-marking policy has not been verified, so
do not claim that all manufacturer variants are unmarked.
