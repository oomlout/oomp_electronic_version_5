# YAGEO MFR50S miniature half-watt display evidence

Reviewed online on 2026-11-18: [YAGEO MFR series specification](https://robu-prod-media.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2025/02/2499275.pdf), covering MFR50S and its five-band 1% marking. The 6.3 mm by 2.4 mm nominal body is represented by KiCad's 6.3 mm by 2.5 mm body and bent leads.

The selected parts are 500 mW, even though their legacy catalogue IDs say quarter watt. The render represents the selected half-watt part; the ID needs separate taxonomy review. Beige coating, band spacing, shades, and bent leads are illustrative.
