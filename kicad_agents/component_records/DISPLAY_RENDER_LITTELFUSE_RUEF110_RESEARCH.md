# Littelfuse RUEF110 display research

The existing local manufacturer drawing `datasheet.pdf`, SHA-256
`cf03cd3a63b2f97721f654e5bb97647656a1a658fba845ff3ca898ea6ca2ad6d`,
is Tyco specification SCD25170 revision G, visually reviewed. It identifies
the exact RUEF110, part marking `U110`, additional manufacturer and lot
markings, epoxy insulation and 24 AWG tin-plated copper-clad steel leads. The
drawing shows an approximately rectangular body with rounded corners, maximum
width 7.4 mm and thickness 3.0 mm, 4.3–5.8 mm lead spacing and minimum lead
length 7.6 mm. The catalogue's 5.08 mm lead pitch is within that range.

The source's 7.4 × 6.6 × 3 mm body is not stated as a set of nominal
dimensions in the drawing; maximum height B of 14.2 mm is measured to the
lead shoulder. A representative face-up display can use rounded rectangular
geometry to distinguish it from RXEF010, but size, edge curvature, lead bends,
yellow colour and exact marking layout are illustrative. `U110` is verified;
manufacturer logo and lot code are omitted. Display model only, not CAD or a
PCB footprint.
