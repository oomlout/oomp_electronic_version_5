# Nexperia 74HC4066BQ DHVQFN14 display research

Nexperia's [74HC/HCT4066 datasheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74HC_HCT4066.pdf) identifies `74HC4066BQ` as SOT762-1 DHVQFN14, a 2.5 × 3 × 0.85 mm body with 14 terminals and an exposed pad. The [manufacturer product page](https://www.nexperia.com/products/analog-logic-ics/analog-switches-and-multiplexers/analog-switches/series/74HC4066-74HCT4066.html) gives 0.5 mm pitch. Review the terminal positions in the [package outline](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/outline-drawing/SOT762-1.pdf) before generating geometry; do not assume a simple seven-per-side arrangement.

[NXP's SOT762-1 package page](https://www.nxp.com/packages/SOT762-1.html) classifies the terminals as quad-positioned. The official KiCad footprint catalog includes `DHVQFN-14-1EP_2.5x3mm_P0.5mm_EP1x1.5mm`; inspect that exact footprint and the manufacturer outline before building the 3D geometry. The `DIL-compatible` package description alone does not mean that all pads lie on two opposite sides.

The installed KiCad footprint confirms five contacts on each long edge and two on each short edge, plus a nominal 1 × 1.5 mm exposed-pad land. Its referenced 3D model is absent locally. `kicad_agents/display_model_nexperia_sot762_1.py` constructs a 2.5 × 3 × 0.85 mm illustrative body with this 14-contact layout, 0.5 mm pitch and an approximate central pad. The terminals extend 0.012 mm beyond each nominal body edge and are shown 0.12 mm high for visibility; their depth, edge shape, pin-one indicator and finish are illustrative. The central pad is based on the footprint land and is not claimed as an exact physical pad. Visually inspect both the marking and model before a final render.
