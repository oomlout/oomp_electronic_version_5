# MaxLinear RS-485 SOIC-8 display evidence

MaxLinear lists the exact SP485EEN-L/TR and SP3485EN-L/TR orderables in NSOIC8 on the product pages linked below. The [SP3485 datasheet](https://www.maxlinear.com/Document/index?id=22069&languageid=1033&partnumber=SP3485&type=Data+Sheets) confirms the package and the industrial temperature grade. The catalogue specifies 4.9 × 3.9 mm body dimensions and the SOIC-8 footprint for both; SP485E also has 1.75 mm height, while SP3485 has no recorded height. The generic KiCad SOIC-8 model is illustrative, not a manufacturer-qualified mechanical model.

Manufacturer sources reviewed do not establish exact top markings for these orderables. The displayed family identifiers are illustrative; suffixes, logo, lot/date codes, typeface and placement are omitted or illustrative.
