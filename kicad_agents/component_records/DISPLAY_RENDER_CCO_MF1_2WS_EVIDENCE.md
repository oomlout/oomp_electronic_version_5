# CCO miniature half-watt metal-film resistors

Reviewed online 2026-11-18: the manufacturer [CCO MF metal-film specification](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/442097c559f2eb401b6de319ad027dae.pdf?productCode=C119329), which describes the MF1/2WS small body as 6.5 ±1 mm long by 2.3 ±0.5 mm diameter, with five-band 1% markings. The generic KiCad 6.3 × 2.5 mm body is within those limits. Beige coating, colour shades and bent leads are illustrative; the manufacturer describes sky-blue coating.

- [MF1/2WS-560Ω±1% T / C129902](https://www.elektromall.com.tr/yurtdisi-urunler/through-hole-resistors-k72?sayfa=27): 560 Ω, 500 mW; green, blue, black, black, brown. The source is a reseller reproducing the selected LCSC listing.
- [MF1/2WS-750Ω±1% T / C129904](https://www.lcsc.com/de/product-detail/Through-Hole-Resistors_CCO-Chian-Chia-Elec-MF1-2WS-750-1-T_C129904.html): 750 Ω, 500 mW; violet, green, black, black, brown.

The legacy catalogue IDs say quarter watt, but both selected parts are half watt. The supplier's prior stored manufacturer numbers were truncated to `T`; the complete identifiers above were restored. The manufacturer PDF was reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed.
