# RALEC RTT051R00FTP 0805 1 Ω display evidence

The selected part is RALEC RTT051R00FTP, 0805, 1 Ω, 1%, 125 mW, as
identified by [LCSC C4321](https://item.szlcsc.com/4728.html). The catalogue
ID unfortunately includes `uni_royal` even though it selects RALEC; it is a
legacy naming conflict, not a manufacturer attribution. Do not describe the
selected part or its datasheet as UNI-ROYAL.

The existing RALEC RTT IE-SP-007 family specification at
`parts_source/electronic_resistor_0402_82_ohm/datasheet.pdf`
(SHA-256 `67185ba26226dc0174363d45383a9708d460eabd8abe820dda63ccbe60fe64f0`) lists RTT05 dimensions on page 4 and describes
four-character 1% RTT05 decimal markings on page 12. Its rules yield `1R00`
for 1 Ω. The 2.00 × 1.20 mm KiCad display geometry lies within the documented
RALEC body tolerance; materials, ink and typeface are illustrative.
