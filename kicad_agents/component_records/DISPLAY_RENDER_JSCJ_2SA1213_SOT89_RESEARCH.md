# JSCJ 2SA1213 SOT-89 display research

The catalogue specifies Jiangsu Changjing Electronics Technology (JSCJ) 2SA1213, gain range 120–240, in SOT-89-3L. The locally stored PDF (SHA-256 `bff4967d4cd2034ff43976a3c98702ba66d79ba8b6ab8a84d2498447c072f26f`) is by Jiangsu Changjiang Electronics Technology (JCST), a different manufacturer. It calls the same gain range Y and shows NY, so it cannot verify JSCJ's marking.

An independently inspected [JSCJ 2SA1213 SOT-89-3L revision 2 datasheet](https://pf.unikeyic.com/datasheet/upload/2022/02/24/2SA1213%20SOT-89-3L%20V2.pdf), hosted by a distributor, gives the Y range as 120–240 and its marking as **NY1**. It specifies body length 4.4–4.6 mm, body width 2.3–2.6 mm, overall width 3.94–4.25 mm and height 1.4–1.6 mm. No online PDF was saved locally.

The KiCad SOT-89 model has approximately 4.5 × 2.5 mm body and 4.0 mm overall width, height 1.52 mm; these dimensions fit the JSCJ outline. Material and text placement are approximate. NY1 is the fixed manufacturer code, while typeface, orientation and production codes are illustrative. This model is for display and is not verified manufacturing CAD.
