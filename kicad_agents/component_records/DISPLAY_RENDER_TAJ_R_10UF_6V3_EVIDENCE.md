# TAJ R-case 10 µF / 6.3 V display pilot

Reviewed 2026-11-19. [KYOCERA AVX's TAJ specification](https://datasheets.kyocera-avx.com/TAJ.pdf)
page 1 gives the R-case 2.05 × 1.30 × 1.20 mm nominal envelope, positive
anode stripe and the small P/R case marking arrangement: voltage code J
for 6.3 V and capacitance code 106 for 10 µF. [DigiKey's exact listing](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/kyocera-avx/TAJR106M006RNJ/808790)
identifies TAJR106M006RNJ as 10 µF / 6.3 V R-case. `J / 106` is an
illustrative layout; actual ink, logo, production code and possible higher
voltage substitute may differ.

The KiCad Kemet R model's overall bounds are 2.0 × 1.2 × 1.2 mm and are
scaled in X and Y to the AVX nominal envelope. Colour, stripe, termination
shape and finish are illustrative. This is a display, not a dimensional or
purchasing specification. No PDF was downloaded.
