# onsemi MM74HCT373MTCX TSSOP20 example evidence

onsemi's [datasheet](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/mm74hct373-d.pdf) lists `MM74HCT373MTCX`, 20-pin TSSOP case 948E. The catalogue says `74HCT373MTCX`, without the `MM` prefix; equivalence is not established, so the render represents the listed MM-prefixed example and does not verify the catalogue MPN. The [case 948E drawing](https://www.onsemi.com/download/package-drawing/pdf/948e.pdf) specifies 6.40–6.60 × 4.30–4.50 mm body, 0.65 mm pitch, and 1.20 mm maximum height. Exact top marking is unconfirmed; `HCT373` is an illustrative partial family identifier. Typeface, orientation, placement, logo and variable date/lot details are illustrative.

The source lists 6.5 × 4.4 × 1.1 mm and TSSOP20 footprint. The generic KiCad model has 1.2 mm overall height, within the onsemi maximum but above the source height. It is a display approximation, not a verified mechanical model or PCB footprint.
