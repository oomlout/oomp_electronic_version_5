Vishay independently identifies IRFBE20PbF, 800 V, 1.8 A, TO-220AB in its [official product datasheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/91117/91117.pdf); its [product page](https://www.vishay.com/en/product/91117/) links the [TO-220AB high-voltage outline 92506](https://www.vishay.com/docs/92506/packageinformation_to-220ab_highvoltage.pdf). The official drawing specifies body length 14.32–15.86 mm, width 9.96–10.36 mm and lead projection 13.40–14.40 mm. These sources were read online; no replacement PDF was saved locally.

The KiCad display model measures approximately 15.09 mm body length, 10.25 mm width and 13.435 mm lead projection, within those limits. The overall model bounds are approximately 10.25 × 4.455 × 28.525 mm. Geometry and finish are approximate; this is not verified manufacturing CAD. IRFBE20 is an illustrative identifier; exact physical marking, typeface, logo and lot/date codes are unverified.
