# Fenghua 0402 low-value C0G display evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-18. Fenghua general series specification FH-2016-001,
reproduced at https://pdf.elecfans.com/p/5746704.html, gives nominal
0402 dimensions 1.00 × 0.50 × 0.50 mm and tolerance code C = ±0.25 pF.
These are family-level dimensions; exact individual thicknesses have not been
independently verified. Source records omit both height and tolerance.

LCSC identifies each exact 50 V C0G part and capacitance:
- 0402CG2R5C500NT, 2.5 pF: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1560.html
- 0402CG6R0C500NT, 6 pF: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1575.html
- 0402CG7R0C500NT, 7 pF: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1577.html
- 0402CG8R0C500NT, 8 pF: https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C1578.html

Generic KiCad geometry and unmarked appearance are illustrative. No PDF was downloaded.
