# MDD HER205 DO-15 display research

The MDD family datasheet SHA-256 `9b810f6b89dd395874c3779b849db05531fdd78beb38bdd09a8c809e13f68d74` page 1 was visually reviewed.
It specifies a 5.8–7.6 mm body, 2.6–3.6 mm body diameter, 0.70–0.86 mm
lead diameter and a polarity band. The marking table lists MDD HER205; the display uses HER205 without the logo, and typography and placement are illustrative. The HER204 source holds the family PDF; HER205 has no local PDF.

The KiCad DO-15 STEP SHA-256
`71d21632cb4f83434a45f5380edd2d52c82ad56284fc49178326aa333117e6ed`
has 11.06 × 3.62 × 6.61 mm overall bounds and bent leads at 10.16 mm pitch,
unlike the straight-lead drawing. Model diameter slightly exceeds the drawing
maximum. Source height 4 mm is not the stated body diameter.
The display is not manufacturer CAD or a PCB footprint. No PDF was downloaded.
