selected supplier or tolerance. These images show specific 1% Yageo RC_L
examples; they do not assign those attributes to the generic entries.

unmarked: https://yageogroup.com/content/datasheet/asset/file/PYU-RC_GROUP_51_ROHS_L
No local PDF is claimed. KiCad geometry and materials are illustrative.
