# TI TL431AC DBZ display evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-20. The catalogue identifier names `TL431ACDBZ` but its source record does not specify manufacturer or a full orderable MPN. TI's [TL431 orderable addendum](https://www.ti.com/ods/sysadd/oa/symlink/tl431_oa.pdf) lists `TL431ACDBZT` in three-pin DBZ SOT-23 with alternative markings `TAC3`, `TACG`, `TACS` and `TACU`. The display represents the T-suffix orderable as an example; the catalogue's missing orderable suffix and manufacturer must not be silently inferred as confirmed. `TAC3` is one valid example, not the only production marking. Typeface, orientation, logo and variable lot/date details are illustrative.

The source specifies 2.9 × 1.6 × 1.1 mm and SOT-23 footprint. The existing KiCad SOT-23 model is 2.5 × 3.0 × 1.2 mm overall; it is scaled to the catalogue's 1.1 mm height as an illustrative display model, not a verified mechanical model or footprint.
