# FOJAN FRP1206F15R0TSN display evidence

[LCSC C2934388](https://www.lcsc.com/de/product-detail/C2934388.html?is_substitute=1&original_product_code=C51048303&original_unit_price=0.0072)
identifies the selected FOJAN FRP1206F15R0TSN as 15 Ω, 1%, 1206,
0.5 W. The manufacturer [FRP high-power series specification](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/lcsc/2504231555_FOJAN-FRP1206F6R80TS_C48533792.pdf)
pages 1–2 gives four-character 1% codes and R as the decimal marker below
100 Ω, so `15R0` denotes 15 Ω. Page 3 specifies a 1206 body length of
3.10 ±0.10 mm; the generic KiCad 3.20 mm display model is at the upper limit.
The generic geometry, materials, ink and placement are illustrative. The
manufacturer PDF was reviewed online; no local PDF is claimed.
